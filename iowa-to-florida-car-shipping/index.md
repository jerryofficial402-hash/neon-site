## How a car gets from Iowa to Florida

Carriers from Iowa usually go east and south: I-80 to the Quad Cities, then south on I-74 and I-65, or I-57 and I-24, to I-75 into Florida. From Des Moines, I-35 to Kansas City and then I-70 east is another option.

Many shipments on this route are snowbirds sending a car to Florida for the winter, plus retirees moving south for good.

### Before you book

- Book southbound winter trips in early fall. October to December is the busiest time.
- Farm and long gravel-drive pickups usually meet at the nearest paved road.
- Florida requires a VIN verification for out-of-state cars at registration.
