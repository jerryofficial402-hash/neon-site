## Quick Answer: How Much Does It Cost to Ship a Car?

The average cost to ship a car in the United States ranges from **$600 to $1,800 for open auto transport** and **$1,000 to $2,600 for enclosed auto transport**. Exact rates depend on route distance (averaging $0.40–$2.00+ per mile), vehicle size, carrier availability, seasonal demand, and pickup date flexibility.

## 3. 6 Key Factors That Affect Your Car Shipping Cost

1. **Distance & Lane Density:** Major interstate corridors (I-95, I-10, I-80) cost less than rural detours.
2. **Vehicle Size & Weight:** Large SUVs, lifted trucks, and oversized vehicles occupy more trailer space and weight.
3. **Open vs. Enclosed Carrier:** Open carries 7–10 cars; enclosed carries 2–6 cars with total weather protection (+40%–60%).
4. **Operable vs. Inoperable Condition:** Non-running vehicles requiring winch loading add $100–$200.
5. **Seasonality:** Summer peak and snowbird seasons affect supply/demand.
6. **Pickup Timing:** Flexible windows (3–5 days) secure optimal rates vs. expedited single-day pickups.

## 7. 6 Tips to Lower Your Car Shipping Cost

1. Be flexible with pickup and delivery dates.
2. Choose open transport when appropriate.
3. Meet the carrier near major highway exits.
4. Remove personal luggage (keep under 100 lbs).
5. Book 1–2 weeks in advance.
6. Ship multiple vehicles together for multi-car discounts.
