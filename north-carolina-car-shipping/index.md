## Tips & Tricks for Saving on Vehicle Shipping

### Off Season Savings

Car shipping rates drop during the off-season. Even shifting your shipment by a few weeks can lead to real savings. Avoid peak moving seasons like summer and major holidays to get the best rates.

### Flexible Pickup & Delivery

Choosing a five-day window for pickup and delivery can lower your shipping cost. Carriers can plan routes more easily with flexible dates, which means more options and better pricing for you.

### Hub City Advantages

For pickups and drop-offs in North Carolina, choose a location near major highways. Spots close to I-40 or near Charlotte attract more carriers, giving you more options and more competitive rates on your shipment.

### Open Trailers

Open trailers cost less than enclosed trailers and work well for most standard vehicles. If your car doesn’t need extra protection from the elements, an open trailer is the smart, budget-friendly choice.

## North Carolina Vehicle Transport FAQs

How do I prepare my vehicle for transit to/from NC? +

Before shipping to or from North Carolina, remove all personal items and take photos to document existing damage. Keep your gas tank at a quarter full, disable alarms, and ensure the vehicle is operational. 

Is my vehicle insured transporting from North Carolina? +

Absolutely. Your vehicle is insured during transport. Every carrier traveling through North Carolina's I-40 corridor and beyond must carry active cargo insurance for any potential damage. 

How far out should I schedule my pickup service in North Carolina? +

For shipments in North Carolina, we advise booking at least 1–2 weeks in advance. During the busy summer moving season or when students are heading to NC colleges, booking 2–4 weeks ahead helps secure your dates. 

How do I pay for my auto transport? +

No payment is due at booking. You simply provide a payment method to secure an NC carrier. The remaining balance can be paid to the driver on delivery day using cash or certified check. 

Can I pack personal items inside my vehicle? +

You can generally pack up to 100 lbs of personal items stored in the trunk or below the window line. Note that carriers leaving NC are not liable for personal items left inside. 

Do I need to be present at both pickup and delivery? +

Yes, you or a trusted representative must be present at your North Carolina pickup and delivery locations to inspect the vehicle and sign the Bill of Lading. 

What's the difference between Open vs Enclosed Auto Transport? +

Open transport is the standard, budget-friendly method where your car rides on an open-air carrier. Enclosed transport shields your vehicle from North Carolina's changing weather and road debris in a covered trailer, making it ideal for luxury cars but costing more. 

## Factors that Affect North Carolina Shipping Prices

### Distance

The farther your car travels, the more you’ll pay. However, longer trips often cost less per mile than shorter ones.

### Route

Shipping along major highways is cheaper and faster. Rural or off-the-beaten-path routes cost more due to limited carrier access.

### Season

Summer and snowbird season drive prices up. Shipping during slower months, like fall or early spring, can save you money.

### Vehicle Type

Larger vehicles like SUVs and trucks take up more space and use more fuel. Expect to pay more than you would for a standard sedan.

### Traffic Patterns

Busy travel weeks, like Thanksgiving, can slow down delivery times. Booking during lighter traffic windows helps keep costs and delays down.

### Carrier Availability

During peak season, carriers fill up fast. You may wait longer for pickup, so book early to lock in your preferred dates.

### Open vs Enclosed

Open trailers are the most affordable option. Enclosed trailers offer extra protection but cost more — ideal for luxury or classic cars.

### Population Density

Shipping to or from a busy city can cost more. Dense urban areas have more traffic and tighter access for large carriers.
