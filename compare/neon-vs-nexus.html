Quick Answer: Neon vs Nexus Auto Transport

Both are licensed brokers that book independent carriers, and neither charges anything at booking. Nexus Auto Transport has been in business since 2015, is a BBB-accredited business with an A rating, and averages 4.2 out of 5 on Trustpilot from 2,355 reviews; it charges a deposit when a driver is assigned. Neon Auto Transport, founded in 2025, has a 5.0 Google rating from 47 reviews, charges $0 until a carrier is assigned, and requires every carrier to carry $500,000 in cargo insurance. Nexus has the longer track record; Neon offers a smaller, hands-on team.

Nexus Auto Transport Reviews: What Customers Say

Nexus has built a large review footprint for a broker of its age. On Trustpilot it averages 4.2 out of 5 from 2,355 reviews, and its BBB profile shows an A rating, accreditation since 2016 and 1,270 customer reviews. The BBB lists 163 complaints over the past three years.

Happy customers often mention responsive agents and smooth deliveries. When reviews are negative, they usually involve the same issues seen across car shipping: prices that rise after booking when no carrier accepts the original rate, missed pickup windows, slow communication, and confusion about whether the deposit is refundable.

None of that is unusual for a busy broker, but it tells you what to ask before you book with anyone: what happens if no carrier takes the quoted price, and exactly when the deposit becomes non-refundable.

Deposits: How Nexus and Neon Charge

Nexus doesn't charge anything when you book. Once a driver is assigned, it charges a deposit to your card and the rest is paid at delivery. Customers have reported deposits of roughly 20–30% of the total, such as about $200–$380 on a $1,100–$1,300 shipment. Some reviewers say they didn't realize the deposit was non-refundable once a carrier was assigned, so ask about it in writing.

Neon Auto Transport charges $0 upfront and nothing until a carrier is assigned to your shipment. Before you book, we explain what is paid to Neon, what is paid to the carrier at delivery and which payment methods the driver accepts. Ask us for the cancellation terms on your order and we'll send them in writing.

Who Should Choose Nexus Auto Transport

  • You're comfortable paying a deposit when your driver is assigned.

Who Should Choose Neon Auto Transport

  • You want a small team that handles your shipment personally, from quote to delivery.
  • You're in Northern Virginia, the D.C. area or Northern California, near our offices in Woodbridge, VA and Live Oak, CA.
  • You want $0 upfront, the driver's direct number after dispatch, and every carrier carrying $500,000 in cargo insurance.

Neon is newer than Nexus and has fewer reviews, and we say so openly. Read our customer reviews, check our authority on the FMCSA SAFER system, and compare our quote side by side with theirs.

Questions to Ask Any Broker Before You Book

  1. When is my card charged, and how much? At booking, at dispatch or at pickup.
  2. Is the deposit refundable, and until when?
  3. What happens if no carrier accepts my price? Will you ask me before raising it?
  4. How much cargo insurance does the carrier carry, and can I see the certificate?
  5. Who do I call during transit, the broker, the driver, or both?

Neon vs Nexus FAQs

Is Nexus Auto Transport legit?

Yes. Nexus is a licensed auto transport broker based in Schaumburg, Illinois, in business since 2015, and a BBB-accredited business with an A rating.

What are Nexus Auto Transport's reviews like?

As of November 2026, Nexus averages 4.2 out of 5 on Trustpilot from 2,355 reviews and has 1,270 reviews on its BBB profile. Negative reviews mostly mention price increases after booking, pickup delays and deposit refunds.

Does Nexus Auto Transport require a deposit?

Not at booking. Nexus charges a deposit to your card once a driver is assigned, and customers report roughly 20–30% of the price. The balance is paid at delivery.

Does Neon Auto Transport require a deposit?

No. Neon charges $0 upfront and nothing until a carrier is assigned to your shipment.

Which is cheaper, Neon or Nexus?

It depends on your route, vehicle and dates. Both book carriers from the same market, so get quotes with identical details and compare what each includes.
