## Nexus Auto Transport Reviews: What Customers Say

Nexus has built a large review footprint for a broker of its age. On **Trustpilot** it averages **4.2 out of 5 from 2,355 reviews**, and its **BBB profile** shows an A rating, accreditation since 2016 and 1,270 customer reviews. The BBB lists 163 complaints over the past three years.

Happy customers often mention responsive agents and smooth deliveries. When reviews are negative, they usually involve the same issues seen across car shipping: **prices that rise after booking** when no carrier accepts the original rate, **missed pickup windows**, slow communication, and confusion about whether the deposit is refundable.

None of that is unusual for a busy broker, but it tells you what to ask before you book with anyone: what happens if no carrier takes the quoted price, and exactly when the deposit becomes non-refundable.

## Deposits: How Nexus and Neon Charge

**Nexus** doesn't charge anything when you book. Once a driver is assigned, it charges a deposit to your card and the rest is paid at delivery. Customers have reported deposits of roughly 20–30% of the total, such as about $200–$380 on a $1,100–$1,300 shipment. Some reviewers say they didn't realize the deposit was non-refundable once a carrier was assigned, so ask about it in writing.

**Neon Auto Transport** charges **$0 upfront** and nothing until a carrier is assigned to your shipment. Before you book, we explain what is paid to Neon, what is paid to the carrier at delivery and which payment methods the driver accepts. Ask us for the cancellation terms on your order and we'll send them in writing.

## Questions to Ask Any Broker Before You Book

1. **When is my card charged, and how much?** At booking, at dispatch or at pickup.
2. **Is the deposit refundable,** and until when?
3. **What happens if no carrier accepts my price?** Will you ask me before raising it?
4. **How much cargo insurance does the carrier carry,** and can I see the certificate?
5. **Who do I call during transit**, the broker, the driver, or both?
