Quick Answer: Neon vs Montway

Both companies are licensed brokers that book independent carriers, and both charge nothing until a carrier is assigned. Montway has been in business since 2006, holds an A+ BBB rating as an accredited business, and offers a 30-day TruePrice Guarantee; on Trustpilot it averages 3.2 out of 5 from more than 2,100 reviews. Neon Auto Transport was founded in 2025, has a 5.0 Google rating from 47 reviews, and gives every customer a small team that handles the shipment from quote to delivery. Choose Montway for scale and track record; choose Neon for a personal, hands-on experience.

Montway Auto Transport Reviews: What the Numbers Say

Montway ships a very large number of cars, so it has far more reviews than most brokers. As of November 2026 it holds an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau, where it has been accredited since 2007 and shows 893 customer reviews. The same BBB profile lists 288 complaints over the past three years, which is common for a company of its size. On Trustpilot, Montway averages 3.2 out of 5 from 2,132 reviews.

Across public review sites, positive reviews tend to mention easy online booking and smooth deliveries, while negative reviews usually involve pickup delays, price changes before dispatch or cancellation fees. Those themes appear across the whole car shipping industry, not just at Montway, and they are worth asking any broker about before you book.

Payment and Cancellation

Montway charges your card when your shipment is dispatched to a carrier. According to U.S. News, a $249 broker fee is non-refundable once a carrier is assigned, though it can be applied to a later Montway order; you can cancel at no cost before a carrier is assigned. The remaining balance is typically paid to the carrier.

Neon Auto Transport also takes $0 upfront: nothing is charged until we've assigned a carrier to your shipment. Before you book, we tell you exactly what is paid to us, what is paid to the carrier at delivery and how. Ask us about cancellation terms for your order and we'll put them in writing.

Price Guarantees

Montway's TruePrice Guarantee says that once your order is booked and confirmed, the price is guaranteed for 30 days, and Montway covers the difference if rates rise; its terms apply to operable, factory-standard vehicles and can exclude hard-to-reach locations. Neon offers a price lock on your quoted rate, so the price you book is the price you pay unless your shipment details change (for example a different vehicle, an inoperable car or a new pickup address). Whichever company you choose, read what the guarantee covers before you rely on it.

We're a newer company, so we have fewer reviews than Montway. We'd rather earn trust one shipment at a time: read our customer reviews, check our FMCSA authority on the SAFER system, and compare our quote with any other broker's.

How to Compare Car Shipping Quotes Fairly

  1. Compare the same details. Same vehicle, running condition, open or enclosed, and pickup window.
  2. Ask what is paid when. Broker fee, carrier payment, and accepted payment methods at delivery.
  3. Read the guarantee and cancellation terms in writing before you book.
  4. Be careful with very low quotes. A price far below others often means a long wait for a carrier willing to take it. See how to find cheap car shipping without getting scammed.
  5. Check the license and insurance of the broker and the carrier on FMCSA SAFER.

Neon vs Montway FAQs

Is Montway Auto Transport legit?

Yes. Montway is a licensed auto transport broker that has been in business since 2006 and holds an A+ rating as a BBB-accredited business. Like any broker, it books independent carriers to move your car.

What are Montway's reviews like?

As of November 2026, Montway averages 3.2 out of 5 on Trustpilot from 2,132 reviews and has 893 reviews on its BBB profile. Complaints mostly involve pickup delays, price changes and cancellation fees.

Is Neon Auto Transport a broker or a carrier?

Neon is a licensed broker (MC 1703787, USDOT 4355879). We book FMCSA-registered carriers, and every carrier we use must carry $500,000 in cargo insurance.

Do Neon and Montway charge a deposit?

Neither charges anything at booking. Montway charges when a carrier is assigned, and its $249 broker fee is non-refundable after that point. Neon charges $0 until a carrier is assigned to your shipment.

Which is cheaper, Neon or Montway?

It depends on the route, vehicle and timing. Both price from the same carrier market, so the best way to know is to get quotes with identical details and compare what each includes.
