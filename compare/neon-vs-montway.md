## Montway Auto Transport Reviews: What the Numbers Say

Montway ships a very large number of cars, so it has far more reviews than most brokers. As of November 2026 it holds an **A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau**, where it has been accredited since 2007 and shows 893 customer reviews. The same BBB profile lists 288 complaints over the past three years, which is common for a company of its size. On **Trustpilot**, Montway averages **3.2 out of 5 from 2,132 reviews**.

Across public review sites, positive reviews tend to mention easy online booking and smooth deliveries, while negative reviews usually involve pickup delays, price changes before dispatch or cancellation fees. ## Payment and Cancellation

**Montway** charges your card when your shipment is dispatched to a carrier. According to U.S. News, a $249 broker fee is non-refundable once a carrier is assigned, though it can be applied to a later Montway order; you can cancel at no cost before a carrier is assigned. The remaining balance is typically paid to the carrier.

**Neon Auto Transport** also takes **$0 upfront**: nothing is charged until we've assigned a carrier to your shipment. Before you book, we tell you exactly what is paid to us, what is paid to the carrier at delivery and how. Ask us about cancellation terms for your order and we'll put them in writing.

## Price Guarantees

Montway's **TruePrice Guarantee** says that once your order is booked and confirmed, the price is guaranteed for 30 days, and Montway covers the difference if rates rise; its terms apply to operable, factory-standard vehicles and can exclude hard-to-reach locations. Neon offers a **price lock** on your quoted rate, so the price you book is the price you pay unless your shipment details change (for example a different vehicle, an inoperable car or a new pickup address). Whichever company you choose, read what the guarantee covers before you rely on it.

## How to Compare Car Shipping Quotes Fairly

1. **Compare the same details.** Same vehicle, running condition, open or enclosed, and pickup window.
2. **Ask what is paid when.** Broker fee, carrier payment, and accepted payment methods at delivery.
3. **Read the guarantee and cancellation terms** in writing before you book.
4. **Be careful with very low quotes.** A price far below others often means a long wait for a carrier willing to take it. See [how to find cheap car shipping without getting scammed](/blog/cheap-car-shipping-without-getting-scammed/).
5. **Check the license and insurance** of the broker and the carrier on FMCSA SAFER.
