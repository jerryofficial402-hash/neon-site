Open vs Enclosed: Auto Transport Comparison

Quick Answer

Direct Answer

Open auto transport averages $0.50–$1.00 per mile ($700–$1,500 coast-to-coast) and carries 90% of vehicles nationwide. Enclosed auto transport costs $1.00–$2.50 per mile ($1,200–$2,800 coast-to-coast) — a 30–60% premium providing 100% weather, rock chip, and road debris protection with hydraulic lift gates and up to $500,000 cargo insurance. Choose open for daily drivers; choose enclosed for classic, luxury, exotic, and low-clearance vehicles.

Which Should You Choose? The Decision Checklist

✓

Choose Open Transport When:

  • Standard Daily Vehicles: Sedans, SUVs, trucks, and minivans used for regular commuting.
  • Vehicle Value Under $70,000: Where standard cargo coverage ($250,000 carrier policy) is fully adequate.
  • Fastest Dispatch Needed: 90% of nationwide carriers are open haulers, ensuring faster pickup scheduling (1–3 days).
  • Budget Optimization: Save $400–$1,100 compared to enclosed transport on long-distance moves.
  • Factory Ground Clearance: Standard clearance vehicles easily navigate open trailer loading ramps.
★

Choose Enclosed Transport When:

  • Exotic, Luxury, or Classic Cars: Ferraris, Lamborghinis, Porsches, vintage restorations, and custom builds.
  • Vehicles Valued Over $100,000: Up to $500,000 active cargo insurance protection per vehicle.
  • Low Ground Clearance: Vehicles with ground effects, splitters, or lowered suspensions requiring hydraulic lift gates.
  • Severe Winter / Coastal Weather: Shielding against winter road salt (I-95/I-80), blizzards, hail, and highway rock chips.
  • Pristine Custom Finishes: Matte wraps, ceramic coatings, or concours-quality paint requiring soft-tie wheel restraints.
