### Moving to California? Your Vehicle Checklist
1. **Register within 20 days:** New residents must register out-of-state vehicles with the California DMV within 20 days of establishing residency — schedule around your delivery date.
2. **Plan for the smog check:** Most vehicles need a smog certification before registration. Model year 1975 and older, electric vehicles, and cars under four model years old are typically exempt.
3. **Check equipment compliance:** California regulates window tint (front side windows must allow over 70% light transmission) and bumper height by vehicle weight class. If your vehicle is modified, verify compliance before it ships.
4. **Be present at delivery:** You or your representative signs the Bill of Lading at delivery — this is your condition record, so inspect before signing.
