Quick Answer • Car Shipping Snapshot

Shipping a standard sedan from North Carolina to Florida typically costs $500 to $850 on open transport during off-season (January–March, July–September), rising to $650–$1,050 during fall snowbird season (October–December). The route covers 400–750 miles depending on your exact pickup and delivery cities, with transit taking 1–4 days after pickup. SUVs and trucks run $150–$250 higher. Enclosed transport adds 30–50% to the base rate.

Why This Is One of the Busiest Southeast Corridors

The North Carolina–Florida lane carries heavy, steady volume for a few specific reasons — and knowing which one applies to you changes what you should expect from timing and pricing:

☀️ Snowbird Migration

Retirees and seasonal residents ship vehicles south every fall (peak: October–December) and back north every spring. This is the single biggest driver of southbound demand and pricing swings on this route.

🎖️ Military PCS Moves

North Carolina hosts Fort Liberty (Fayetteville) and Camp Lejeune/MCAS New River (Jacksonville, NC) — both regularly rotate personnel to Florida bases like MacDill AFB (Tampa), NAS Jacksonville, and NAS Pensacola. Ask about active-duty discounts.

🎓 College Relocations

Students moving to or from UF, FSU, UCF, Duke, UNC, and NC State ship vehicles at the start and end of each semester, concentrating demand around August and May.

🌴 Permanent Relocation & Dealers

Florida's no-income-tax status continues to draw permanent movers from North Carolina year-round. Additionally, Charlotte and Raleigh dealer networks regularly move inventory to Florida markets.

How Long Does It Take?

Most North Carolina to Florida shipments complete in 1–4 days of actual transit, plus a 1–2 day pickup window after booking — so plan on 3–6 days from booking to delivery in most cases. Shorter routes (Charlotte/Wilmington to north Florida) run on the faster end; longer routes to south Florida (Miami, Fort Lauderdale) or from western NC (Asheville) take closer to 4 days.

Hurricane Season Note: Between June and November, named storms occasionally force carriers to reroute around Florida, which can add 1–2 days to delivery. This affects a minority of shipments, but build in a buffer during storm months.

What to Watch Out For (Common Complaints on This Route)

Car shipping has a reputation problem, and it's worth addressing directly:

  • Lowball quotes: A price noticeably below every other quote is usually a red flag — it's often a number no carrier will actually accept, followed by a price hike closer to pickup.
  • Upfront deposits before carrier assignment: Legitimate brokers don't require full payment before your vehicle is picked up. Be cautious of anyone asking for a large deposit with no carrier confirmed yet.
Neon Auto Transport requires $0 upfront deposit and gives you a price-lock guarantee at booking — the quote you receive is the quote you pay.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to ship a car from North Carolina to Florida?

Most shipments cost $500 to $850 on open transport during off-season, and $650 to $1,050 during fall snowbird season (October–December). Exact pricing depends on your specific route, vehicle size, and season.

How long does it take to ship a car from NC to FL?

Transit typically takes 1 to 4 days, depending on the exact pickup and delivery cities. Add a 1–2 day pickup window after booking for a realistic door-to-door timeline of 3–6 days.

Is it cheaper to ship my car or drive it myself?

For most people, shipping is cheaper once you account for gas, food, lodging, wear on the vehicle, and the value of your time on a 400–750 mile drive. Shipping also avoids adding mileage to a car.

Do you ship non-running vehicles from North Carolina to Florida?

Yes. Non-running vehicles require a carrier with a winch or hydraulic lift gate, which is a standard request on this route — just let us know when booking so we assign the right equipment.

Is my vehicle insured during transit?

Yes. Every carrier in our network carries active cargo insurance, and Neon Auto Transport provides up to $500,000 in coverage from pickup to delivery.

Does hurricane season affect delivery to Florida?

Occasionally. Between June and November, carriers may reroute around active storms, which can add 1–2 days to delivery. Most shipments during this window are unaffected, but build in a small buffer.

Do I need to be present for pickup and delivery?

You or an authorized adult (18+) needs to be present to hand off the keys and sign the Bill of Lading at pickup, and to inspect the vehicle and sign the Proof of Delivery at drop-off.

Can I ship my car during a military PCS move from North Carolina to Florida?

Yes. We regularly handle PCS shipments between North Carolina bases (Fort Liberty, Camp Lejeune) and Florida bases (MacDill AFB, NAS Jacksonville, NAS Pensacola), including coordination around government paperwork.
