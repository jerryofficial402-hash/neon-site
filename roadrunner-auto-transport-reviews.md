## Quick Answer: Is RoadRunner Auto Transport Good?

**RoadRunner Auto Transport is a real, licensed car shipping broker** with about 30 years in business and thousands of customer reviews. U.S. News rates it **4.1 out of 5**, and Move.org's weighted score across 4,814 Trustpilot, BBB and Yelp reviews is **4.5 out of 5**. Its quotes for open transport are often at or below average, and there's no deposit at booking. The most common complaints are **price increases after booking, slow communication and carriers arriving later than expected**, and its online quotes and dates aren't guaranteed. It's a reasonable choice if you get the final price in writing before a carrier is assigned.

**Note:** Neon Auto Transport is a car shipping broker and competes with RoadRunner. We've kept this review to facts published by RoadRunner and independent reviewers, with sources at the bottom, and we say where RoadRunner does better than us.

## What RoadRunner Customers Say

Reviews vary a lot by site. U.S. News describes them as mostly positive on Trustpilot and mostly negative on Yelp. Reading across the reviews and the summaries by U.S. News and Move.org, the same themes come up again and again:

### What people like

- Easy online quote and booking
- Fast pickup on busy routes
- Carriers who communicate and send updates
- 24/7 online tracking
- Wide range of vehicles, including boats, RVs and heavy equipment

### Common complaints

- Price went up after booking
- Slow or unclear communication
- Carrier arrived later than the expected date
- Cancellation fee charged before a carrier was hired
- Hard to reach a person outside business hours

These complaints aren't unique to RoadRunner. They're the most common complaints about car shipping brokers in general, because a broker's quote is an estimate of what a carrier will accept. If no carrier takes the job at that price, the price goes up or the pickup slips. That's why it matters to ask what happens if no carrier accepts your price (more below).

## Is RoadRunner Auto Transport Legit?

Yes. RoadRunner is a long-running, federally registered broker with thousands of verified reviews across several sites, and major reviewers such as U.S. News and Move.org recommend it for certain customers. "Legit" doesn't mean every shipment goes smoothly, though; the complaints above show where things can go wrong with any broker. Before you book with RoadRunner or anyone else:

1. **Look up the license** on the [FMCSA SAFER](https://safer.fmcsa.dot.gov/) website, and check the assigned carrier's insurance before pickup.
2. **Get the total price in writing**, including what's paid to the broker and what's paid to the driver at delivery.
3. **Ask what happens if no carrier accepts the price** — will they ask you for more, or wait?
4. **Ask about cancellation** before you sign: is there a fee, and when does it apply?

## RoadRunner Auto Transport FAQs

### Is RoadRunner Auto Transport legit?

Yes. It's a federally registered car shipping broker with about 30 years in business and thousands of customer reviews. Reviews are mixed, with complaints mostly about price increases after booking, communication and late carriers.

### What is RoadRunner Auto Transport's rating?

U.S. News rates it 4.1 out of 5 (October 2026). Move.org's weighted score across 4,814 Trustpilot, BBB and Yelp reviews is 4.5 out of 5.

### How much does RoadRunner Auto Transport cost?

Typically about $900–$1,500 for open transport. U.S. News received quotes of $835 for 500 miles, $925 for 1,500 miles and $1,745 for 2,500 miles for a sedan. Enclosed quotes tend to be higher than average.

### Does RoadRunner require a deposit?

No deposit is required at booking. Payment is due once your shipment is booked with a carrier.

### Does RoadRunner charge a cancellation fee?

Move.org reports a $135 cancellation fee may apply, and U.S. News notes the policy isn't clearly stated on RoadRunner's website. Ask for the cancellation terms in writing before you book.

### Is RoadRunner BBB accredited?

Sources disagree. U.S. News reported in October 2026 that RoadRunner was not BBB-accredited, while RoadRunner's website advertises BBB A+ accreditation. Check its current profile on bbb.org.
