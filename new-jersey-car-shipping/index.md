## New Jersey Vehicle Transport FAQs

How much does it cost to ship a car to New Jersey? +

It depends on distance, vehicle type, and season, but most routes fall between $150 (short regional trips) and $1,700+ (cross-country). Get an instant quote for an accurate number. 

How long does car shipping to or from New Jersey take? +

Regional trips (under 300 miles) often take 1–3 days. Cross-country routes typically take 5–10 days depending on carrier scheduling and route density. 

Is open or enclosed transport better for New Jersey shipments? +

Open transport is fine for most everyday vehicles. [Enclosed transport](/services/enclosed-car-shipping/) is better for classic, luxury, or low-clearance cars that need extra protection. 

Do I need to be present for pickup and delivery? +

No, but someone authorized needs to be available to inspect and sign off on the vehicle at both ends. 

Should I empty my car before shipping? +

Yes. Remove personal belongings — most carriers don't insure items left inside the vehicle. 

Is door-to-door car shipping available everywhere in New Jersey? +

In most areas, yes. In dense cities like Hoboken or Jersey City, drivers may ask to meet at a nearby wide-access location instead of directly at your door. 

How far in advance should I book? +

1–3 weeks is typical; book earlier during peak snowbird season (fall and spring). 

Can I ship a car that doesn't run? +

Yes, but you'll need to disclose this upfront since it requires special loading equipment. 

What documents do I need to ship my car? +

Vehicle registration, ID, and the signed inspection report at pickup and delivery. 

Does car shipping insurance cover damage? +

Licensed carriers carry cargo insurance for transport-related damage. Always confirm coverage details before booking. 

Can I ship a car to a college campus? +

Yes — many families use this service for out-of-state college moves. 

What's the difference between a broker and a carrier? +

A broker arranges shipping with a network of carriers; a carrier physically transports the vehicle. Make sure both are properly licensed. 

Is it cheaper to ship multiple cars at once? +

Often, yes — dealers and families moving multiple vehicles can sometimes get better per-vehicle rates. 

How is pricing calculated? +

Primarily by distance, vehicle size/weight, transport type (open/enclosed), season, and current fuel prices. 

Can military members get flexible scheduling? +

Yes, many providers, including Neon Auto Transport, work with PCS timelines and deployment schedules. 

What happens if my car is damaged during transport? +

You file a claim against the carrier's cargo insurance, supported by the pickup and delivery inspection reports. 

Do I need to winterize my car before shipping in winter? +

Not typically required, but a full tank isn't necessary — a quarter tank is standard. 

Can I track my shipment? +

Most reputable providers offer status updates throughout transit; ask about tracking when you book. 

Is New Jersey to Florida a popular shipping route? +

Yes — it's one of the most heavily traveled snowbird corridors in the country. 

What's the best time of year to ship a car to New Jersey? +

Outside of peak snowbird season (Sept–Nov and Mar–May), pricing and availability tend to be more flexible.
