## Factors that Affect Louisiana Shipping Prices

### Distance

The farther your car travels, the more you’ll pay. However, longer trips often cost less per mile than shorter ones.

### Route

Shipping along major highways is cheaper and faster. Rural or off-the-beaten-path routes cost more due to limited carrier access.

### Season

Summer and snowbird season drive prices up. Shipping during slower months, like fall or early spring, can save you money.

### Vehicle Type

Larger vehicles like SUVs and trucks take up more space and use more fuel. Expect to pay more than you would for a standard sedan.

### Traffic Patterns

Busy travel weeks, like Thanksgiving, can slow down delivery times. Booking during lighter traffic windows helps keep costs and delays down.

### Carrier Availability

During peak season, carriers fill up fast. You may wait longer for pickup, so book early to lock in your preferred dates.

### Open vs Enclosed

Open trailers are the most affordable option. Enclosed trailers offer extra protection but cost more — ideal for luxury or classic cars.

### Population Density

Shipping to or from a busy city can cost more. Dense urban areas have more traffic and tighter access for large carriers.

## Tips & Tricks for Saving on Vehicle Shipping

### Off Season Savings

Car shipping rates drop during the off-season. Even shifting your shipment by a few weeks can lead to real savings. Avoid peak moving seasons like summer and major holidays to get the best rates.

### Flexible Pickup & Delivery

Choosing a five-day window for pickup and delivery can lower your shipping cost. Carriers can plan routes more easily with flexible dates, which means more options and better pricing for you.

### Hub City Advantages

For pickups and drop-offs in Louisiana, choose a location near major highways. Spots close to I-10 or near New Orleans attract more carriers, giving you more options and more competitive rates on your shipment.

### Open Trailers

Open trailers cost less than enclosed trailers and work well for most standard vehicles. If your car doesn’t need extra protection from the elements, an open trailer is the smart, budget-friendly choice.

## Louisiana Vehicle Transport FAQs

How do I prepare my vehicle for transit to/from LA? +

Remove personal items and take photos of any existing damage. Louisiana experiences high humidity, heavy rains, and hurricane seasons, so ensure your windows and sunroof are fully sealed. Keep the gas tank at a quarter full, disable alarms, and ensure it runs properly. 

Is my vehicle insured transporting from Louisiana? +

Yes, your vehicle is fully insured during transport. Carriers traveling across the Pelican State on I-10 or I-20 are required by law to maintain active cargo insurance for any damages that may occur. 

How far out should I schedule my pickup service in Louisiana? +

For shipments in or out of Louisiana, booking 1–2 weeks in advance is ideal. During the busy hurricane evacuation season or peak summer moving months, booking 2–4 weeks ahead helps secure an available carrier. 

How do I pay for my auto transport? +

No upfront payment is required. You provide a payment method to lock in your carrier. You can pay the balance by card, or hand cash or a certified check directly to the driver upon delivery. 

Can I pack personal items inside my vehicle? +

You can pack up to 100 lbs of personal items in the trunk or below the window line. Carriers are not responsible for these items, so remove all valuables before your car leaves Louisiana. 

Do I need to be present at both pickup and delivery? +

Yes, you or a trusted representative must be present at pickup and delivery in Louisiana to inspect the vehicle and sign the Bill of Lading, which serves as the official condition report. 

What's the difference between Open vs Enclosed Auto Transport? +

Open transport is economical but exposes your car to Louisiana's unpredictable weather. Enclosed transport shields your vehicle from heavy rains and debris in a covered trailer, making it ideal for luxury or classic cars.
