Car Shipping in Georgia: The Complete 2026 City, Route & Cost Guide

A Neon Auto Transport guide to shipping a car to or from Atlanta, Savannah, Augusta, Columbus, Macon, Athens, Marietta, Warner Robins, Albany & Brunswick

Georgia sits at the crossroads of the Southeast's freight network, making it one of the most active car shipping states in the country. Atlanta's I-75/I-85/I-20 interchange is the single busiest logistics junction in the Southeast, Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport is the world's busiest airport by passenger traffic, and the Port of Brunswick's Colonel's Island Terminal is one of the largest vehicle-import (roll-on/roll-off) ports in the United States.

Add in a fast-growing population, several major military installations, and a booming corporate-relocation economy, and it's easy to see why so many customers search for car shipping cost estimates on Georgia routes every day.

This guide breaks down the ten highest-demand Georgia metro areas for vehicle shipping, real route distances between them and major national hubs, and exactly how Neon Auto Transport moves vehicles in and out of the state — whether you need open carrier transport for a daily driver or enclosed shipping for a luxury or classic vehicle.

Why Is Georgia a Top Car Shipping State?

Georgia consistently ranks among the busiest Southeastern states for inbound and outbound vehicle shipments, driven by its position as an interstate crossroads, a major international vehicle-import hub, and a concentration of military installations that generate steady PCS-driven demand year-round.

  • ✓
    Interstate crossroads: Atlanta sits on I-75, I-85, and I-20 — the primary north-south and east-west freight corridors for the entire Southeast, giving carriers constant capacity through the state.
  • ✓
    Vehicle import hub: The Port of Brunswick's Colonel's Island Terminal is one of the busiest roll-on/roll-off auto-import terminals in the U.S., processing hundreds of thousands of vehicles a year for major manufacturers.
  • ✓
    Military relocations: Fort Moore (Columbus), Fort Eisenhower (Augusta), Robins Air Force Base (Warner Robins), and Fort Stewart (near Savannah) generate steady PCS-driven shipping demand statewide.
  • ✓
    Corporate and population growth: Metro Atlanta is one of the fastest-growing metro economies in the country, with major corporate relocations and a large film/production industry driving both personal and commercial vehicle shipments.
  • ✓
    College towns: Athens (University of Georgia) sees predictable seasonal shipping spikes at the start and end of each academic year.

Georgia's Top 10 Cities for Car Shipping

Each city below is ranked by shipping volume and carrier network density across Neon Auto Transport's Georgia lanes. Every profile leads with key logistics data for cost planning.

1. Atlanta — Georgia's Capital & Southeast Logistics Hub

Atlanta is Georgia's #1 car shipping market, with the shortest pickup windows and most competitive per-mile pricing in the state, and transit times of just 2–4 days from New York. Sitting at the junction of I-75, I-85, and I-20, it's one of the easiest cities in the country to get a carrier into or out of quickly, and Hartsfield-Jackson's global connectivity drives heavy corporate relocation and dealer-to-dealer demand.

Major Routes I-75, I-85, I-20, I-285 (the Perimeter)

2. Savannah — Georgia's Historic Coastal Port City

Savannah's coastal I-95 access makes it one of the most cost-efficient Georgia cities to ship to, with transit from New York typically running 2–4 days. The Port of Savannah (Garden City Terminal) is one of the busiest container ports in the country, and nearby Fort Stewart in Hinesville adds consistent military relocation volume.

Major Routes I-95, I-16
Avg. Transit from NY 2–4 days
Avg. Transit from LA 6–8 days
Local Shipping Note Coastal I-95 access makes Savannah an efficient stop on Northeast-to-Florida carrier runs, often lowering costs versus more inland Georgia cities.

3. Augusta — East Georgia's Riverside Military & Medical Hub

Augusta blends a large medical/university economy with Fort Eisenhower's steady PCS-driven shipping demand, with transit from New York typically running 2–4 days. Its position near the South Carolina border makes it a natural stop for East Coast carrier routes.

Car Shipping Cost to Georgia: What Drives the Price

Cost to ship a car to Georgia ranges from $150 for short in-state moves to $1,600+ for cross-country routes from the West Coast, depending on distance, vehicle type, method, and season. There's no single flat rate — auto transport quotes are built from several variables, outlined below.

Factors That Change Your Price

  1. Distance and route popularity — high-traffic lanes through Atlanta on I-75/I-85/I-20 are typically cheaper per mile than remote or rural drop-off points.
  2. Vehicle size and weight — SUVs, trucks, and oversized vehicles cost more than a standard sedan.
  3. Open vs. enclosed shipping — enclosed transport runs roughly 40–60% more than open carrier transport, but protects the vehicle from road debris and weather — important for luxury and classic car shipping.
  4. Seasonality — PCS move season (May–August) and UGA move-in/move-out windows drive up demand and prices on specific Georgia lanes.
  5. Timing flexibility — expedited shipping (guaranteed pickup dates) costs more than a flexible pickup window.
  6. Vehicle condition — shipping non-running cars requires winch-equipped trailers and typically adds a fee.

Looking for the cheapest option? Budget transport is almost always open-carrier, terminal-to-terminal, with a flexible pickup date. For a hands-off experience, door-to-door shipping remains the most popular choice among Georgia customers.

How to Choose a Trustworthy Georgia Auto Transport Company

Georgia's high shipping volume unfortunately also attracts scams — low-ball quotes that balloon after booking, brokers with no real carrier network, or unlicensed haulers. Verify these five things before you book with anyone:

  1. FMCSA licensing — every legitimate carrier and broker must hold an active USDOT and MC number, searchable on the FMCSA's SAFER database.
  2. Cargo insurance — confirm active insured transport coverage and ask for a certificate of insurance before pickup.
  3. Vetted, background-checked haulers — Neon Auto Transport works exclusively with vetted carriers meeting minimum safety-rating and insurance thresholds.
  4. Verified reviews — check Google, BBB, and Transport Reviews, not just testimonials on the company's own site.
  5. Transparent, binding quotes — a trustworthy quote process locks your price at booking, with no last-minute increases.

What is a Bill of Lading, and why does it matter?

It's the legal document that records your vehicle's condition at pickup and delivery, signed by both you and the driver. It's the single most important piece of paper if a damage dispute ever comes up, so read it before signing — both times.

Car Shipping Insurance Requirements

Federal law requires all interstate auto transport carriers to carry cargo insurance, though coverage minimums vary by carrier size. Always request proof of insured transport coverage before your vehicle is loaded, and confirm whether your own auto policy provides any supplemental coverage during transit.

Frequently Asked Questions: Shipping a Car to Georgia

How much does it cost to ship a car to Georgia? +
Average cost ranges from $150 for short in-state moves to $1,600+ for cross-country routes from the West Coast. Get an exact number with a free quote based on your specific pickup and delivery cities.
What is the cheapest way to ship a car to Georgia? +
Open car carrier, terminal-to-terminal transport with a flexible pickup window is typically the most budget-friendly option.
How long does it take to ship a car to Atlanta or Savannah? +
From the Northeast, expect 2–4 days. From the West Coast, expect 5–7 days. In-state moves (e.g., Atlanta to Macon) usually complete within 1 day.
Is door-to-door or terminal-to-terminal car shipping better for Georgia? +
Door-to-door is more convenient and the most popular option; terminal-to-terminal is a good cost-saving alternative if you're near a major terminal hub like Atlanta or Savannah.
Can I ship a car internationally through Georgia's ports? +
Yes. The Port of Savannah and the Port of Brunswick's Colonel's Island Terminal both handle international vehicle import and export, making Georgia one of the best-connected states for international car shipping.
Which Georgia city is cheapest and fastest to ship to? +
Atlanta typically has the shortest pickup windows and most competitive per-mile pricing in the state, since it sits at the junction of I-75, I-85, and I-20 and hosts the widest carrier network.
Does Neon Auto Transport handle military PCS moves in Georgia? +
Yes — pickup and delivery windows are coordinated around PCS orders for service members near Fort Moore, Fort Eisenhower, Robins Air Force Base, Marine Corps Logistics Base Albany, and Fort Stewart.
What documents do I need for car shipping? +
The bill of lading (provided by the carrier), vehicle registration or title, photo ID, and any applicable power-of-attorney if someone else is dropping off or receiving the vehicle.
Is my vehicle insured during transport? +
Yes. Federal law requires interstate carriers to carry cargo insurance; always confirm active coverage and request a certificate of insurance before pickup.
How do I know if a Georgia auto transport company is legitimate? +
Verify their USDOT and MC number on the FMCSA's SAFER database, confirm active cargo insurance, and check third-party reviews on Google, BBB, or Transport Reviews rather than relying only on the company's own site.
When is Georgia's busiest car shipping season? +
May through August (PCS move season) and the UGA move-in/move-out windows in August and May are the tightest capacity periods — book 2–3 weeks ahead during these times.
Can you ship a non-running car in Georgia? +
Yes, with a winch-equipped carrier arranged in advance; disclose the vehicle's condition upfront, as it affects carrier assignment and price.
Do you ship RVs and motorhomes in Georgia? +
Yes — demand is especially high from the coastal markets near Savannah and Brunswick.
Is enclosed shipping worth the extra cost in Georgia? +
For daily drivers, usually not. For vehicles valued above roughly $75,000, classic and collector cars, or Augusta's Masters Tournament-week traffic, the 40–60% premium is generally worth it.
