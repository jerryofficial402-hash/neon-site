## Tips & Tricks for Saving on Vehicle Shipping

### Off Season Savings

Car shipping rates drop during the off-season. Even shifting your shipment by a few weeks can lead to real savings. Avoid peak moving seasons like summer and major holidays to get the best rates.

### Flexible Pickup & Delivery

Choosing a five-day window for pickup and delivery can lower your shipping cost. Carriers can plan routes more easily with flexible dates, which means more options and better pricing for you.

### Hub City Advantages

For pickups and drop-offs in Iowa, choose a location near major highways. Spots close to I-80 or near Des Moines attract more carriers, giving you more options and more competitive rates on your shipment.

### Open Trailers

Open trailers cost less than enclosed trailers and work well for most standard vehicles. If your car doesn’t need extra protection from the elements, an open trailer is the smart, budget-friendly choice.

## Iowa Vehicle Transport FAQs

How do I prepare my vehicle for transit to/from IA? +

Remove personal belongings and take photos of existing damage. Since Iowa can experience severe thunderstorms and heavy winter snow, ensure your car is weather-ready. Keep the gas tank about a quarter full, disable alarms, and verify it's operational. 

Is my vehicle insured transporting from Iowa? +

Yes, your vehicle is fully insured during transport. Every carrier navigating I-80 or I-35 through the Hawkeye State is required to carry active cargo insurance for transit protection. 

How far out should I schedule my pickup service in Iowa? +

For Iowa shipments, especially around Des Moines or Cedar Rapids, book 1–2 weeks in advance. During extreme winter weather or peak summer moves, booking 2–4 weeks out ensures preferred dates. 

How do I pay for my auto transport? +

No payment is required at booking. You provide a payment method to secure your carrier. The balance can be paid by card, or you can pay the driver directly at delivery with cash or a certified check. 

Can I pack personal items inside my vehicle? +

You can pack up to 100 lbs of personal items in the trunk or below the window line. Carriers are not responsible for these items, so avoid packing valuables while traversing Iowa's rolling plains. 

Do I need to be present at both pickup and delivery? +

Yes, you or a trusted adult must be present at pickup and delivery in Iowa to inspect the vehicle and sign the Bill of Lading, which acts as the official condition report. 

What's the difference between Open vs Enclosed Auto Transport? +

Open transport is budget-friendly but exposes your car to Iowa's elements like rain or snow. Enclosed transport provides a covered trailer, protecting against harsh midwestern weather and road debris, ideal for luxury cars. 

## Factors that Affect Iowa Shipping Prices

### Distance

The farther your car travels, the more you’ll pay. However, longer trips often cost less per mile than shorter ones.

### Route

Shipping along major highways is cheaper and faster. Rural or off-the-beaten-path routes cost more due to limited carrier access.

### Season

Summer and snowbird season drive prices up. Shipping during slower months, like fall or early spring, can save you money.

### Vehicle Type

Larger vehicles like SUVs and trucks take up more space and use more fuel. Expect to pay more than you would for a standard sedan.

### Traffic Patterns

Busy travel weeks, like Thanksgiving, can slow down delivery times. Booking during lighter traffic windows helps keep costs and delays down.

### Carrier Availability

During peak season, carriers fill up fast. You may wait longer for pickup, so book early to lock in your preferred dates.

### Open vs Enclosed

Open trailers are the most affordable option. Enclosed trailers offer extra protection but cost more — ideal for luxury or classic cars.

### Population Density

Shipping to or from a busy city can cost more. Dense urban areas have more traffic and tighter access for large carriers.
