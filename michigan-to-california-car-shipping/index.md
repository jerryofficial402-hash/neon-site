## How Long Does It Take to Ship a Car from Michigan to California?

Pickup usually happens **1–5 days after you book**, and the drive takes **5–9 days**. Carriers leave Michigan on I-94 through Chicago, then head west on one of these routes:

- **I-80** across Iowa, Nebraska, Wyoming, Utah and Nevada — the direct route to Sacramento and the Bay Area.
- **I-80 to I-76 and I-70** through Denver and Utah, then I-15 into Las Vegas and Southern California.
- **I-55 and I-44 to I-40** through St. Louis and Oklahoma City — the southern route carriers favor in winter.

From December through March, lake-effect snow in West Michigan, storms in Wyoming and chain controls on Donner Pass are the main causes of delay. More in our [car shipping transit times guide](/car-shipping-transit-times/).

## Buying a Car in Michigan for California? Check Emissions First

Michigan follows federal emission standards, not California's. Many cars sold at Michigan dealerships are certified for 49 states, which matters if you're a California resident buying a newer car here — for example with auto-industry employee pricing, from a family member, or from a Michigan dealer.

- **Newer cars with fewer than 7,500 miles** generally can't be registered in California unless they're certified to California emission standards.
- **Check the emissions label under the hood** for California or "50-state" certification, or ask the dealer to confirm from the VIN before you buy.
- **Moving to California with a car you already own?** That usually isn't a problem; you'll still need a smog check and registration when you arrive.

We pick up from dealerships, private sellers and auction yards across Michigan. Have the bill of sale and any gate pass ready for the driver.

## Before Pickup in Michigan

- **Wash off road salt, underneath too.** Michigan winters leave salt that hides scratches and speeds up rust. A clean car makes the Bill of Lading inspection accurate and arrives in better shape.
- **Plan for snow.** In winter, clear the driveway and make sure the car starts; a 75-foot carrier can't get down unplowed side streets, so the driver may meet you on a main road.
- **Collector cars:** Michigan ships a lot of classics and restorations west. Choose an enclosed carrier to keep salt, rain and road debris off the paint.
- **Take dated photos** of every side, leave about a quarter tank of fuel, and remove personal items — they aren't covered by the carrier's cargo insurance.

## Best Time to Ship a Car from Michigan to California

- **Late spring and early fall** (April–May, September–October) usually bring the best mix of price and reliable weather.
- **Summer** is the busiest moving season, with students and job relocations; book 2–3 weeks ahead.
- **Winter** can be cheaper, but snow in Michigan and on the western passes can slow pickup and transit.
- **Collector car season** wraps up in the fall, when many owners ship classics west for the winter. Book enclosed carriers early.

## When Your Car Arrives in California

- **Inspect the car with the driver** and note any new damage on the Bill of Lading before you sign. See [how car shipping insurance works](/insurance/).
- **Register within 20 days** of becoming a California resident; the DMV does a VIN verification on cars from other states.
- **Pass a smog check.** Most vehicles need one; gasoline cars from 1975 or older and electric vehicles are exempt.
- **Get a California license within 10 days** and move your insurance to a California policy.

## Michigan to California Car Shipping FAQs

### How much does it cost to ship a car from Michigan to California?

About $1,150–$1,600 for a sedan on an open carrier and $1,650–$2,300 enclosed. The Bay Area and Sacramento run about $50 more, and SUVs or pickups add $100–$350.

### How much does it cost to ship a car from Detroit to Los Angeles?

About $1,150–$1,600 on an open carrier for the roughly 2,280-mile trip, with delivery usually 5–8 days after pickup.

### How long does it take to ship a car from Michigan to California?

Usually 5–9 days after pickup, plus 1–5 days to schedule pickup after you book. Winter storms can add a day or two.

### Can I register a car bought in Michigan in California?

Usually, but check emissions first. California generally won't register a vehicle with fewer than 7,500 miles unless it's certified to California emission standards. Look for a California or "50-state" emissions label under the hood.

### Is it cheaper to drive or ship a car from Michigan to California?

Driving costs about $850–$1,250 in fuel, hotels and food over 4–5 days. Shipping costs $1,150–$1,600 plus a flight, so the totals are close, and shipping avoids winter driving and 2,300 miles on your car.

### Should I ship a classic car from Michigan enclosed?

Yes. Enclosed carriers keep classics and restorations out of road salt, snow and debris. They cost about 1.4–1.6 times as much as open transport, and there are fewer of them, so book early.
