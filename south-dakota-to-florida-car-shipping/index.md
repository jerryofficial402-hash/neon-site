## How a car gets from South Dakota to Florida

From Sioux Falls, carriers usually go south on I-29 to Kansas City, then east and south through St. Louis and Nashville, and finally down I-75 into Florida. From Rapid City, I-90 east to Sioux Falls comes first.

Snowbirds make up most of this route, along with retirees moving south. Florida and South Dakota are also both popular with full-time RVers.

### Before you book

- Book southbound winter trips in early fall. Carriers fill up from October to December.
- Western South Dakota pickups take longer to schedule. Meeting on I-90 helps.
- Florida requires a VIN verification for out-of-state cars at registration.
