Quick Answer • Route Snapshot

Shipping a standard sedan from Maryland to Florida (car transport MD to FL) typically costs $700 to $1,150 on open transport, covering 900–1,050 miles depending on your exact pickup and delivery cities. Transit takes 2–4 days after pickup, plus a 1–2 day pickup window — so plan on 3–6 days from booking to delivery. Shipping from Florida back to Maryland runs a comparable range, generally $750 to $1,200, with the exact number depending on season and current carrier capacity in each direction. If you're asking more generally how much it costs to ship any car to Florida, most East Coast origin states fall in the $400–$1,300 range depending on distance — Maryland sits toward the higher end of that range because of the roughly 1,000-mile distance involved.

Why Maryland-to-Florida Is a High-Volume Corridor

☀️ Snowbird Migration

DC-metro and Baltimore-area retirees ship vehicles south for the winter, with the heaviest southbound volume from January through March — enough that it's known to cause noticeable I-95 congestion through Virginia and the Carolinas during that window.

🌱 Spring Return Shipments

A pattern specific to this corridor: a large share of Maryland/DC-area residents who relocate to Florida for part of the year ship their car back home each spring rather than drive the ~1,000 miles themselves. It's often cheaper than two days of gas and hotels, and puts zero extra miles on the car.

🎖️ Military & Federal Moves

Maryland is home to Fort Meade (NSA/Cyber Command) and sits next to Joint Base Andrews — both regularly rotate personnel toward Florida installations like MacDill AFB, NAS Jacksonville, and NAS Pensacola.

🚗 Baltimore Auctions & Federal Relocations

Baltimore-area auto auctions move regular volume into Florida's large retail resale market. Additionally, the DC-Baltimore corridor sends a steady year-round stream of federal transfers to Florida.

How to Ship a Car to Florida

This section answers the general "how do I actually do this" question, whether you're shipping from Maryland or anywhere else:

  1. Get a quote: Enter your pickup and delivery ZIP codes, vehicle make/model/year, and preferred dates.
  2. Book with no upfront deposit: A carrier is matched to your route — on high-volume lanes like Maryland to Florida, this typically happens within 24–48 hours.
  3. Pickup: The carrier calls ahead, arrives at your pickup location, and you complete a walk-around inspection together before signing the Bill of Lading.
  4. Transit: Your vehicle travels via open or enclosed carrier, generally along I-95.
  5. Delivery: The carrier delivers to your Florida address, you inspect the vehicle together, and sign the Proof of Delivery to complete the shipment.

If your delivery address is a Florida location the carrier can't physically reach — a gated community, a downtown area with truck restrictions — your driver will coordinate a nearby meeting point in advance rather than surprise you with it on delivery day. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to ship a car from Maryland to Florida?

Most shipments cost $700 to $1,150 on open transport, depending on your exact route, vehicle size, and season. Enclosed transport for classic or luxury vehicles typically runs 30–50% higher.

How much to ship a car from Florida to Maryland?

The reverse route generally runs $750 to $1,200 on open transport — a similar range to Maryland-to-Florida, with the exact figure depending on season and which direction has more available carrier capacity at the time you book. See our Florida to Maryland car shipping guide for details.

How to ship a car to Florida?

Get a quote with your pickup and delivery ZIP codes and vehicle details, book with no upfront deposit, and a carrier is assigned to your shipment. The carrier picks up your vehicle, transports it via open or enclosed trailer, and delivers to your Florida address with a signed inspection at both pickup and delivery.

How much does it cost to ship a car to Florida?

It depends heavily on your origin state and distance. Most East Coast routes to Florida run $400 to $1,300. From Maryland specifically, expect $700 to $1,150 for the roughly 1,000-mile trip. Use our instant calculator above for an exact quote from your ZIP code, or check our Florida Car Shipping Hub.

How long does it take to ship a car from Maryland to Florida?

Transit typically takes 2 to 4 days after pickup, plus a 1–2 day pickup window — so plan on 3–6 days from booking to delivery for most routes.

Does hurricane season affect delivery to Florida?

Yes, occasionally. Between June and November, carriers may reroute or pause around active named storms, which can add 1–2 days. Booking with a small buffer during August–October is a good idea.

Is my vehicle insured during transit?

Yes. Every carrier in our network carries active cargo insurance, and Neon Auto Transport provides up to $500,000 in coverage from pickup to delivery.

Do I need to be present for pickup and delivery?

You or an authorized adult (18+) needs to be present to sign the Bill of Lading at pickup and the Proof of Delivery at drop-off.
