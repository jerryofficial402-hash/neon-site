Open vs. Enclosed Transport in Arlington

Arlington sees a high volume of both standard commuters and luxury vehicles. We offer both open and enclosed carrier options to match your vehicle's needs.

Open Transport

Arlington, VA Car Shipping FAQs

How much does it cost to ship a car from Arlington, VA?

Car shipping from Arlington typically costs between $1.00 and $1.40 per mile for open transport. A short regional route like Arlington to New York costs $500–$650. A cross-country route to California costs $1,250–$1,650.

How long does it take to ship a car from Arlington?

Transit time depends on distance. Regional East Coast routes take 1–2 days. Mid-range routes like Arlington to Texas take 3–5 days. Cross-country routes to California take 6–9 days.

Do you offer door-to-door pickup in Arlington neighborhoods like Ballston and Rosslyn?

Yes. We provide door-to-door pickup throughout Arlington, including Clarendon, Ballston, Rosslyn, Crystal City, Pentagon City, and Shirlington.

Do I need to pay a deposit to ship my car from Arlington?

No. Neon Auto Transport does not require any upfront deposit. You pay nothing until a carrier is assigned to your shipment.
