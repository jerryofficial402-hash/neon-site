## How a car gets from Hawaii to Texas

Shipping a car from Hawaii to Texas takes two legs. First the car sails to a West Coast port, usually in Southern California. Then a carrier truck drives it east on I-10 or I-40 to your city in Texas.

Many shipments on this route are military families moving from Hawaii to bases in Texas, such as Fort Hood, Fort Bliss or Joint Base San Antonio, along with civilians relocating.
