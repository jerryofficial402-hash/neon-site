How Car Shipping Works

Shipping a vehicle doesn't have to be complicated. Learn step-by-step how door-to-door auto transport works from getting an instant quote to vehicle delivery and inspection.

Neon Auto Transport LLC is a licensed auto transport broker operating under MC #1703787 and USDOT #4355879.

1

Request a Quote & Select Transport Type

First, submit your pickup and delivery ZIP codes, vehicle make and model, and preferred shipping timeframe. You can select between Open Auto Transport (the most cost-effective method) or Enclosed Car Shipping (ideal for luxury, classic, or exotic vehicles).

Use our Car Shipping Cost Calculator to estimate pricing for your route before requesting your personalized binding quote.

2

Carrier Dispatch & Pickup Scheduling

Once your quote is accepted, Neon Auto Transport dispatches your shipment to a 100% vetted motor carrier operating on your route. All carriers in our network undergo USDOT safety rating verification and carry active cargo insurance ($100,000–$250,000+).

Your assigned driver contacts you 24 hours prior to pickup to confirm your pickup window and arrange accessible door-to-door loading points.

3

Inspection, Transit & Safe Handoff

At pickup, you and the driver complete a detailed visual inspection recorded on the Bill of Lading (BOL). Upon arrival at your destination, perform a final walk-around inspection, verify vehicle condition, sign the BOL, and receive your keys.

How to Prepare Your Vehicle for Transport

  • Clean your vehicle inside and out for an accurate inspection.
  • Remove all personal items (up to 100 lbs permitted in the trunk).
  • Ensure gas tank is around 1/4 full to reduce transport weight.
  • Disable automatic alarm systems and note any existing cosmetic blemishes.
