## How a car gets from Michigan to California

From Michigan, carriers usually take I-94 to Chicago and then I-80 west toward Northern California. Southern California trips often run I-70 to Denver and then I-70 and I-15, or go south to I-40.

Engineers and auto industry workers, students, and families relocating keep this route busy. So do Michigan buyers and sellers of collector cars.

### Before you book

- Winter in Michigan and on I-80 through Wyoming can slow trips from December to February.
- California requires most cars registered there for the first time to pass a smog check.
- Consider enclosed transport for collector cars and fresh restorations.
