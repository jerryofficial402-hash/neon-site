      Key Factors That Determine Your Quote

      📍

      1. Mileage & Distance

      Per-mile transport costs drop significantly on longer coast-to-coast trips compared to short regional moves.

      🚗

      2. Vehicle Type & Size

      Sedans use standard carrier space; larger SUVs, trucks, and modified vehicles require extra trailer capacity.

      🚛

      3. Carrier Type

      Open transport offers maximum savings; enclosed transport provides 100% weather-shielded protection for exotics.

      🗓️

      4. Seasonality & Supply

      Summer relocation months and winter snowbird migrations affect carrier demand along major interstate corridors.

      Cost Calculator FAQs

      How is my auto shipping quote calculated? +
      Your quote is calculated based on total route distance (miles), vehicle size/weight (sedan vs SUV vs truck), vehicle condition (operable vs inoperable), transport type (open vs enclosed), and live carrier supply and demand along the route.
      Are there any hidden fees or upfront deposits required? +
      No. At Neon Auto Transport we require zero upfront deposit when requesting a quote or booking. You pay nothing until a carrier is assigned and pickup is scheduled.
      What is the difference between open and enclosed auto transport? +
      Open transport carries vehicles on a multi-car open trailer — it is the most popular and budget-friendly option. Enclosed transport protects vehicles inside a fully enclosed trailer, ideal for classic, exotic, or high-value vehicles.
      How far in advance should I book my vehicle shipment? +
      Booking 1 to 2 weeks in advance yields the most competitive pricing and flexible pickup options. However, we also accommodate rush and expedited shipments.
