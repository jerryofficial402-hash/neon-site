      PRICING FORMULA

      How Auto Transport Pricing Works: The Cost-per-Mile Formula

      Why car shipping costs decrease per mile as total distance increases.

      When calculating vehicle shipping costs, the most fundamental metric is cost per mile. However, auto shipping does not charge a flat rate per mile across all distances. Car carriers operate with fixed trip costs — including loading and unloading time, commercial bridge and highway tolls, driver rest periods, and equipment wear.

      Because of these fixed overheads, short-distance moves (under 500 miles) require higher per-mile rates to remain economically viable for carriers, while cross-country routes (over 1,500 miles) enjoy significantly lower per-mile rates.

      PRICE DRIVERS

      6 Critical Factors That Determine Your Car Shipping Cost

      Every vehicle shipment has unique variables that impact the carrier's operating cost.

      📍

      1. Distance & Lane Density

      Shipping between major metro hubs connected by major interstates (like I-95, I-10, or I-80) costs less than shipping to rural areas or mountain towns that require carrier detours and deadhead miles.

      🚙

      2. Vehicle Size & Weight

      Car haulers operate under strict 80,000 lb federal highway weight caps. Heavy SUVs, full-size trucks, roof racks, and lifted tires occupy more physical footprint and fuel capacity than compact sedans.

      🛡️

      3. Open vs. Enclosed Trailer

      Open transport accommodates 7–10 cars per trailer, lowering cost. Enclosed transport carries 2–6 cars with total weather/debris shielding, costing 40%–60% more.

      ⚙️

      4. Operable vs. Inoperable

      Vehicles that cannot start, steer, or brake require a specialized winch, auxiliary power, and additional carrier labor during loading and unloading, typically adding $100 to $200.

      ☀️

      5. Seasonality & Weather

      Auto shipping follows seasonal migration patterns. High summer relocation demand and the autumn/spring "snowbird" season (Northeast to Florida/Arizona) cause temporary rate surges on specific corridors.

      ⏱️

      6. Pickup Timing & Flexibility

      Allowing a standard 3–5 day pickup window allows dispatchers to match your vehicle with the most cost-efficient carrier. Requiring guaranteed exact-day or expedited transport commands premium carrier spot rates.

      MONEY-SAVING GUIDE

      6 Proven Ways to Lower Your Car Shipping Cost

      1

      Be Flexible with Your Dates

      Allowing a 3–5 business day pickup window allows dispatchers to negotiate with carriers filling final trailer spots, saving you 5% to 15%.

      2

      Choose Open Auto Transport

      Unless shipping a rare collector car or high-end exotic, open carrier transport provides exceptional safety at a 40%–60% lower cost than enclosed.

      3

      Meet at an Accessible Major Highway Hub

      If you live on a narrow residential street, cul-de-sac, or gated community, meeting the driver at a nearby shopping plaza or rest area avoids access surcharges.

      4

      Remove Heavy Personal Cargo

      Carriers face strict DOT axle weight inspections. Keep trunk items under 100 lbs and reduce gas in the fuel tank to 1/4 full to prevent weight fees.

      5

      Book 1 to 2 Weeks in Advance

      Advance planning secures standard board rates and avoids last-minute rush expedited premiums.

      6

      Ship Multiple Vehicles Together

      Transporting two or more vehicles on the same route earns multi-vehicle carrier discounts (typically $75 to $150 off per vehicle).

      FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

      Car Shipping Cost & Calculator FAQs

      How much does it cost to ship a car across the country?

      Shipping a car coast-to-coast (typically 2,000 to 2,800 miles) averages between $1,100 and $2,000 for standard open auto transport. Enclosed transport for the same distance generally costs $1,600 to $2,800 depending on vehicle dimensions and seasonal route demand.

      Why do car shipping calculators give price ranges instead of exact figures?

      Auto transport pricing is dynamic. Carrier rates fluctuate based on spot market diesel prices, weekly lane volume, driver availability, and seasonal surges. A price range provides an accurate benchmark, while a firm locked quote is finalized once your exact travel dates and vehicle specs are matched with an FMCSA carrier.

      What is the cheapest way to ship a car?

      The cheapest method is standard open multi-car transport with a flexible 3–5 day pickup window, booking 1–2 weeks ahead, and arranging pickup/drop-off near major interstate highways to avoid remote residential access fees.

      Does vehicle size and modification affect auto transport prices?

      Yes. Full-size trucks, large SUVs, lifted vehicles, roof racks, and dual-rear-wheel trucks occupy more trailer space and weight, which increases the shipping rate by $100 to $350 compared to standard sedans.

      Are there extra fees for inoperable vehicles?

      Yes. If a vehicle rolls, steers, and brakes but cannot start under its own engine power, the carrier must use an onboard winch and extra labor, adding approximately $100 to $200 to the shipment.

      Is personal cargo allowed inside the vehicle, and does it cost extra?

      Carriers generally allow up to 100 lbs of personal items secured below window level in the trunk at no extra charge. Excessive cargo is not covered by carrier insurance and can trigger overweight fines at state DOT weigh stations.

      When do I pay for my car shipment, and is a deposit required upfront?

      No deposit is required when booking. Your broker deposit is only charged once a carrier is officially dispatched to your route. The remaining carrier balance is paid directly upon delivery inspection.
