## Shipping a car in Oregon

Most Oregon car shipping is along I-5, which runs from Portland through Salem and Eugene down to California. I-84 heads east from Portland up the Columbia River Gorge toward Idaho.

Portland, Salem and Eugene are the easiest pickup cities. Bend has grown fast and is served regularly, though it sits over the Cascades. The coast and eastern Oregon take more planning.

### Good to know in Oregon

- Cascade passes, like Santiam Pass on the way to Bend, can require chains in winter and may slow carriers.
- Portland's inner neighborhoods have narrow streets and tight parking. Meet on a wider road.
- Oregon has no sales tax, so many buyers have cars shipped here after a purchase.

## When to book in Oregon

Summer is the busiest moving season. Winter snow in the Cascades and the Siskiyou Pass on I-5 can delay trips to and from California.

## Registering a car in Oregon

New Oregon residents register with the Oregon DMV. Check what that office needs from you before your car arrives.

## Oregon car shipping questions

### How long does it take to ship a car from California to Portland?

From the Bay Area, usually two to four days after pickup. From Southern California, three to five days.

### Can you pick up in Bend?

Yes. Bend is a regular stop, but winter passes can add a day. Book a little ahead.
