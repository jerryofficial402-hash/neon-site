## How a car gets from Mississippi to Florida

From the Mississippi Gulf Coast, carriers take I-10 east through Mobile and the Florida Panhandle, then I-75 or I-4 to Central and South Florida. From Jackson, the usual route is I-20 or I-59 east, then I-65 and I-10, or I-75, south.

Snowbirds, retirees and military families make up much of this route, along with people moving along the Gulf Coast for work.

### Before you book

- Hurricane season, June to November, can affect both ends of this route.
- Florida condo buildings often have garages carriers cannot enter. Plan to meet out front.
- Florida requires a VIN verification for out-of-state cars at registration. Ask your county tax collector how to get it done.
