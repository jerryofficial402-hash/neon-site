## Tips & Tricks for Saving on Vehicle Shipping

### Off Season Savings

Car shipping rates drop during the off-season. Even shifting your shipment by a few weeks can lead to real savings. Avoid peak moving seasons like summer and major holidays to get the best rates.

### Flexible Pickup & Delivery

Choosing a five-day window for pickup and delivery can lower your shipping cost. Carriers can plan routes more easily with flexible dates, which means more options and better pricing for you.

### Hub City Advantages

For pickups and drop-offs in Illinois, choose a location near major highways. Spots close to I-90 or near Chicago attract more carriers, giving you more options and more competitive rates on your shipment.

### Open Trailers

Open trailers cost less than enclosed trailers and work well for most standard vehicles. If your car doesn’t need extra protection from the elements, an open trailer is the smart, budget-friendly choice.

## Illinois Vehicle Transport FAQs

How do I prepare my vehicle for transit to/from IL? +

Clean your vehicle and remove all personal belongings. Document any existing damage. For Illinois winters, ensure your antifreeze levels are topped off and the battery is in good condition to prevent issues during cold transport. 

Is my vehicle insured transporting from Illinois? +

Yes, comprehensive insurance coverage is included. Carriers operating on I-90, I-80, or I-55 in Illinois carry active cargo insurance to fully protect your vehicle while in transit. 

How far out should I schedule my pickup service in Illinois? +

We recommend booking your auto transport 1 to 2 weeks ahead for regular shipments. If shipping during the busy summer months or snowbird season out of Chicago, booking 2-4 weeks ahead is best. 

How do I pay for my auto transport? +

No payment is due when booking. Once a carrier is dispatched, you can pay the balance by credit card or pay the driver upon delivery using cash or a certified check. 

Can I pack personal items inside my vehicle? +

You may pack up to 100 lbs of personal items safely secured in the trunk. However, carriers are not liable for any lost or damaged personal items inside the vehicle. 

Do I need to be present at both pickup and delivery? +

Yes, you or an appointed representative must be present to review the vehicle's condition and sign the Bill of Lading at both the pickup and delivery locations. 

What's the difference between Open vs Enclosed Auto Transport? +

Open transport is the most economical way to ship. Enclosed transport provides a fully covered trailer, keeping your vehicle safe from Illinois rain, snow, and road debris, highly recommended for luxury or classic vehicles. 

## Factors that Affect Illinois Shipping Prices

### Distance

The farther your car travels, the more you’ll pay. However, longer trips often cost less per mile than shorter ones.

### Route

Shipping along major highways is cheaper and faster. Rural or off-the-beaten-path routes cost more due to limited carrier access.

### Season

Summer and snowbird season drive prices up. Shipping during slower months, like fall or early spring, can save you money.

### Vehicle Type

Larger vehicles like SUVs and trucks take up more space and use more fuel. Expect to pay more than you would for a standard sedan.

### Traffic Patterns

Busy travel weeks, like Thanksgiving, can slow down delivery times. Booking during lighter traffic windows helps keep costs and delays down.

### Carrier Availability

During peak season, carriers fill up fast. You may wait longer for pickup, so book early to lock in your preferred dates.

### Open vs Enclosed

Open trailers are the most affordable option. Enclosed trailers offer extra protection but cost more — ideal for luxury or classic cars.

### Population Density

Shipping to or from a busy city can cost more. Dense urban areas have more traffic and tighter access for large carriers.
