How the Route Works: Florida to California on I-10

Most carriers take Interstate 10, which runs from Jacksonville to Los Angeles through Tallahassee, Mobile, New Orleans, Houston, San Antonio, El Paso, Tucson and Phoenix. Cars picked up in Miami or Fort Lauderdale join it after I-95 or I-75, which adds a few hundred miles but does not change the price much, because South Florida is the busiest pickup market in the state.

Federal hours-of-service rules limit truck drivers to 11 hours of driving per day, and a loaded car hauler usually covers 400–500 miles a day. Carriers also make delivery stops in Texas and Arizona on the way west. That is why a 2,700-mile trip takes about a week, and why we quote 6–10 days instead of a single date. Our car shipping transit times guide explains the timeline in more detail.

Pickup in Florida usually happens within 1–5 days of booking with a flexible window. If you need the car picked up within 24–48 hours, ask for expedited auto transport.

Best Time to Ship a Car from Florida to California

  • Spring (March–May) costs the most. Thousands of snowbirds ship their cars out of Florida at the same time, so trucks leaving the state fill up fast and outbound prices rise. Book 2–3 weeks ahead in spring. Our snowbird car shipping guide covers this season.
  • Fall and early winter are usually cheapest. From October to January, carriers arrive in Florida full of northern cars and need loads heading back out, which makes rates from Florida more competitive.
  • Hurricane season can delay pickups. It runs June through November and peaks August through October. When a storm is coming, carriers pause Florida and Gulf Coast pickups until roads reopen. Give yourself a few spare days if you ship then.

Before Pickup in Florida

  • Wash the car. Pollen, salt air and lovebug season leave a film that hides scratches. A clean car makes the Bill of Lading inspection accurate.
  • Take out your SunPass or E-PASS transponder. Otherwise you can be billed for tolls the carrier drives through with your car on the trailer.
  • Plan for a 75-foot truck. Many Florida gated communities and HOAs don't allow car haulers inside. Meeting the driver at a nearby large parking lot is normal and keeps the pickup on schedule.
  • Empty the car and leave about a quarter tank of fuel. Carrier cargo insurance covers the vehicle, not personal items inside it. Turn off the alarm and remove loose accessories.
  • Take dated photos of all sides before the driver arrives, and keep your copy of the Bill of Lading.

When Your Car Arrives in California

  • Inspect it with the driver and compare it to the pickup report before you sign the delivery Bill of Lading.
  • Register within 20 days. New California residents must register an out-of-state vehicle with the California DMV within 20 days of establishing residency. The DMV also does a VIN verification on vehicles coming from another state.
  • Plan for a smog check. Most vehicles need one before registration. Exceptions include gasoline cars from 1975 or older and electric vehicles.
  • Switch your insurance from your Florida policy to a California policy once you move.

Florida to California Car Shipping FAQs

How much does it cost to ship a car from Florida to California?

About $1,200–$1,650 for a sedan on an open carrier and $1,750–$2,300 enclosed. Northern California runs about $50 more, and SUVs or pickups add $100–$350.

How long does it take to ship a car from Florida to California?

Usually 6–10 days after pickup for Southern California and 7–10 days for the Bay Area or Sacramento. Pickup typically happens 1–5 days after booking.

What is the cheapest time to ship a car from Florida to California?

Fall and early winter, roughly October through January, when carriers arriving in Florida need loads heading out. Spring is the most expensive because of snowbird demand.

Do I have to be there at pickup and delivery?

You or someone you trust who is 18 or older needs to be there to inspect the car with the driver and sign the Bill of Lading. It doesn't have to be the owner.

Can I pack things in the car?

It's best to ship the car empty. Carrier cargo insurance covers the vehicle, not personal belongings, and extra weight can raise the price. Ask us before pickup if you need to leave something in the trunk.

Do I need a smog check when my car arrives in California?

Most vehicles need a smog check before California registration. Gasoline cars from 1975 or older and electric vehicles are exempt. New residents have 20 days to register.
