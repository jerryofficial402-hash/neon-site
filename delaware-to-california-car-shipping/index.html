How a car gets from Delaware to California

From Delaware, carriers usually head west on I-70 through Pennsylvania and the Midwest to Denver and Utah, then take I-15 into Southern California. Some go south on I-81 to I-40 instead. Northern California trips often use I-80.

Dover AFB families, finance and pharma workers, and families relocating keep this coast-to-coast route active.

Before you book

  • Dover AFB families can plan pickup around PCS dates.
  • California requires most cars registered there for the first time to pass a smog check.
  • Winter weather in the Rockies and Sierra can add a day or two.
