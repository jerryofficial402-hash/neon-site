Understanding Pickup Windows vs. In-Transit Delivery Time

One of the most common points of confusion for vehicle owners is the distinction between carrier assignment, the pickup window, and actual transit time:

1. Pickup Window Coordination

Before transit begins, a licensed broker coordinates carrier assignment based on route availability and your preferred dates. Once assigned, the motor carrier provides a 1-to-5-day pickup window to collect your vehicle.

2. In-Transit Delivery Time

In-transit time begins official tracking only after your vehicle is physically loaded onto the truck and signed for on the Bill of Lading (BOL).

Federal Hours-of-Service (HOS) Regulations & Safety

Commercial motor vehicle drivers operating multi-car carriers are governed by strict Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) Hours-of-Service (HOS) regulations. Drivers are legally permitted to drive a maximum of 11 hours following 10 consecutive hours off-duty.

These safety mandates ensure drivers are alert and well-rested. As a result, carrier schedules account for mandatory rest breaks, fuel stops, weigh station inspections, and loading stops for other vehicles on the trailer.

Key Factors That Impact Delivery Schedules

Weather & Road Conditions

Severe storms, heavy snow, ice, or hurricane detours require drivers to slow down or hold safely until roads clear.

Traffic & Highway Detours

Metropolitan congestion, construction zones, and accidents can add travel time along major interstate corridors.

Multi-Vehicle Trailer Stops

Carriers carrying 7 to 10 vehicles schedule drop-offs and pickups along the route, which requires time for careful unloading and reloading.

Residential Street Access

Narrow streets, low tree branches, or local truck bans may require meeting at a nearby accessible parking lot.

Open vs. Enclosed Transport & Expedited Delivery Options

Open Auto Transport trailers move higher vehicle volumes, offering frequent route dispatching across high-demand corridors. Enclosed Car Shipping provides covered protection from weather and road debris for luxury, classic, or exotic vehicles.

Customers needing priority collection can request expedited coordination options. Expedited scheduling prioritizes carrier assignment for an earlier pickup window, though road safety and federal HOS driving limits still apply.
