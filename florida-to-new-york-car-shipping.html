Quick Answer: Shipping a Car from Florida to New York

Shipping a sedan from Florida to New York costs about $800–$1,150 on an open carrier for most of the year and $1,000–$1,450 during the spring snowbird rush (March–May); enclosed transport runs about $1,200–$1,700. Miami to New York City is about 1,280 miles and usually takes 3–5 days after pickup on I-95. Fall and early winter are the cheapest months to ship north. Why the Price Changes So Much by Season

Florida to New York is the classic snowbird route, and car carriers follow the snowbirds. That makes the same trip much cheaper or more expensive depending on the month:

How Long Does It Take to Ship a Car from Florida to New York?

Pickup usually happens 1–5 days after you book (longer in the spring peak), and the trip takes 2–5 days. Almost every carrier runs Interstate 95 north through Georgia, the Carolinas, Virginia, Maryland and New Jersey. Cars from Tampa, Fort Myers and Naples usually take I-75 to I-10 and join I-95 near Jacksonville.

The last stretch through New Jersey and into New York City is the slowest part of the route because of traffic and tolls, so many drivers plan their New York deliveries for early morning. See our car shipping transit times guide.

Before Pickup in Florida

  • Condo garages and gated communities: a 75-foot car carrier can't fit in a parking garage and many HOAs don't allow it through the gate. Plan to meet the driver at the entrance or a nearby large lot.
  • Remove your SunPass or E-PASS so you're not charged for tolls along I-95.
  • Wash off pollen and salt air before the Bill of Lading inspection so any existing scratches are easy to see.
  • Leaving the car in storage until pickup? Check the battery and tires; cars that sit all summer in Florida heat often need a jump.
  • Empty the car — personal items aren't covered by the carrier's cargo insurance — and leave about a quarter tank of fuel.

Delivery in New York City, Long Island and Upstate

  • New York City: big carriers can't use the parkways or many narrow streets in Manhattan, Brooklyn and Queens. Your driver will call ahead to meet you on a wide avenue or in a large parking lot nearby.
  • Long Island, Westchester and New Jersey: usually true door-to-door delivery.
  • Upstate New York (Albany, Syracuse, Rochester, Buffalo): easy access off the interstates, but winter storms can delay a delivery by a day. Spring deliveries are rarely affected.
  • Have someone there — you or anyone 18 or older can inspect the car with the driver and sign the delivery Bill of Lading.

Moving to New York? Registering a Florida Car

  • Register within 30 days. New York requires new residents to register an out-of-state vehicle within 30 days.
  • New York insurance first. You'll need proof of insurance from a company licensed in New York before the DMV will register the car.
  • Inspection within 10 days. After registering, the car needs a New York safety and emissions inspection within 10 days.
  • Driver's license within 30 days of becoming a resident.

Snowbirds who keep Florida as their legal home usually keep their Florida registration and don't need to re-register in New York. Our New York car shipping guide covers more about shipping in and out of the state.

Florida to New York Car Shipping FAQs

How much does it cost to ship a car from Florida to New York?

About $800–$1,150 for a sedan on an open carrier for most of the year, and $1,000–$1,450 during the March–May snowbird peak. Enclosed transport runs about $1,200–$1,700.

How much does it cost to ship a car from Miami to New York City?

About $850–$1,150 on an open carrier outside the spring peak, for the roughly 1,280-mile trip. Delivery usually takes 3–5 days after pickup.

How long does car transport from Florida to New York take?

Usually 2–5 days after pickup, plus 1–5 days to schedule pickup after you book. Allow more time in spring, when snowbird demand fills trucks.

When is the cheapest time to ship a car from Florida to New York?

October through January. Carriers arrive in Florida full of snowbird cars and need loads heading north, so prices drop. March through May is the most expensive time.

Do I need to register my Florida car in New York?

If you're moving to New York, yes: register within 30 days with New York insurance, then pass a New York inspection within 10 days. Snowbirds who keep Florida as their legal home usually keep their Florida registration.

Is it cheaper to drive or ship a car from Florida to New York?

Driving is cheaper, about $400–$600 for fuel, a hotel night and food. Shipping costs $800–$1,150 but saves two long days on I-95 and 1,280 miles on your car.

Do I have to pay a deposit?

No. Neon Auto Transport charges $0 upfront and nothing until a carrier is assigned to your shipment.
