## Shipping a car in Maine

Most Maine car shipping is along the southern coast and I-95: Portland, South Portland, Saco, Biddeford and Lewiston. I-95 and I-295 continue north to Augusta and Bangor.

Southern Maine is close to Boston and easy for carriers. Northern and eastern Maine are far from other hubs and take longer to schedule.

### Good to know in Maine

- Maine has one of the largest snowbird populations in New England. Book Florida trips early in the fall.
- Coastal islands and peninsulas are hard for large carriers. Meet on the mainland near US-1 or I-95.
- In Maine, you pay excise tax at your town office before the BMV issues your registration.

## When to book in Maine

The busiest stretches are October to December (south to Florida) and March to May (back to Maine). Winter storms can delay northern pickups.

## Registering a car in Maine

New Maine residents register with your municipal office (for excise tax) and the Maine BMV. Check what that office needs from you before your car arrives.

## Maine car shipping questions

### How do I register a car after shipping it to Maine?

Pay the excise tax at your town or city office, then complete the registration with the Maine BMV.

### Can you pick up in northern Maine?

Yes. It takes longer to schedule, so book ahead or meet in Bangor or another town on I-95.
