## Tips & Tricks for Saving on Vehicle Shipping

### Off Season Savings

Car shipping rates drop during the off-season. Even shifting your shipment by a few weeks can lead to real savings. Avoid peak moving seasons like summer and major holidays to get the best rates.

### Flexible Pickup & Delivery

Choosing a five-day window for pickup and delivery can lower your shipping cost. Carriers can plan routes more easily with flexible dates, which means more options and better pricing for you.

### Hub City Advantages

For pickups and drop-offs in Maine, choose a location near major highways. Spots close to I-95 or near Portland attract more carriers, giving you more options and more competitive rates on your shipment.

### Open Trailers

Open trailers cost less than enclosed trailers and work well for most standard vehicles. If your car doesn’t need extra protection from the elements, an open trailer is the smart, budget-friendly choice.

## Factors that Affect Maine Shipping Prices

### Distance

The farther your car travels, the more you’ll pay. However, longer trips often cost less per mile than shorter ones.

### Route

Shipping along major highways is cheaper and faster. Rural or off-the-beaten-path routes cost more due to limited carrier access.

### Season

Summer and snowbird season drive prices up. Shipping during slower months, like fall or early spring, can save you money.

### Vehicle Type

Larger vehicles like SUVs and trucks take up more space and use more fuel. Expect to pay more than you would for a standard sedan.

### Traffic Patterns

Busy travel weeks, like Thanksgiving, can slow down delivery times. Booking during lighter traffic windows helps keep costs and delays down.

### Carrier Availability

During peak season, carriers fill up fast. You may wait longer for pickup, so book early to lock in your preferred dates.

### Open vs Enclosed

Open trailers are the most affordable option. Enclosed trailers offer extra protection but cost more — ideal for luxury or classic cars.

### Population Density

Shipping to or from a busy city can cost more. Dense urban areas have more traffic and tighter access for large carriers.

## Maine Vehicle Transport FAQs

How do I prepare my vehicle for transit to/from ME? +

Remove personal items and photograph existing damage. Because Maine experiences harsh, snowy winters and coastal elements, ensure your vehicle has proper antifreeze and weather protection. Keep the gas tank at a quarter full and ensure it's operational. 

Is my vehicle insured transporting from Maine? +

Yes, your vehicle is fully insured. All carriers traveling along I-95 through the Pine Tree State are required to carry active cargo insurance to cover any transport-related damage. 

How far out should I schedule my pickup service in Maine? +

Booking 1–2 weeks in advance is recommended for Maine. However, if shipping during the heavy winter months or the peak fall foliage season, booking 2–4 weeks ahead helps secure better rates and dates. 

How do I pay for my auto transport? +

There is no payment due at booking. You simply provide a payment method to lock in your carrier. You can pay the balance by card, or with cash or a certified check directly to the driver at delivery. 

Can I pack personal items inside my vehicle? +

Yes, you can pack up to 100 lbs of personal items in the trunk or below the window line. Note that carriers are not responsible for these items, so keep valuables out of the car. 

Do I need to be present at both pickup and delivery? +

Yes, you or a designated adult must be present at both ends of the transport in Maine to inspect the vehicle and sign the Bill of Lading, which serves as your condition receipt. 

What's the difference between Open vs Enclosed Auto Transport? +

Open transport is the most popular and affordable, but exposes your car to Maine's snow and road salt. Enclosed transport provides a covered trailer, offering maximum protection from the elements, ideal for classic or luxury vehicles.
