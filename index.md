## How Long Does Car Shipping Take?

Most car shipments take several days after pickup. Regional routes may take about 1–4 days in transit, mid-distance interstate shipments often take about 3–7 days, and cross-country routes can take approximately 7–14 days after pickup. 

Pickup timing and transit time are different. Before transit begins, a carrier must be assigned and a pickup window coordinated. Weather, traffic, route demand, loading stops, vehicle type, and pickup or delivery access can affect the actual schedule.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to ship a car?

+

Car shipping costs vary by total distance, vehicle size, transport type (open or enclosed), fuel prices, and seasonal carrier demand. You can estimate pricing for your route using our [Estimate My Car Shipping Cost](/cost-calculator/) calculator or request a customized quote on our [Free Car Shipping Quote](/car-shipping-quote/) page. 

### How long does car shipping take?

+

Transit timing begins after pickup and depends on route mileage, weather, traffic, and federal Hours-of-Service rules. Regional moves often take several days, while long cross-country routes take approximately 1 to 2 weeks. Learn more on our [How Car Shipping Works](/how-it-works/) guide. 

### Is Neon Auto Transport a broker or carrier?

+

Neon Auto Transport LLC is a licensed auto transport broker operating under MC #1703787 and USDOT #4355879. We coordinate vehicle shipments through independently owned motor carriers that physically transport your vehicle. Review our brokerage model on our [Why Choose Neon Auto Transport](/why-neon/) page. 

### Is open or enclosed transport right for my vehicle?

+

[Open Auto Transport](/services/open-auto-transport/) is the most popular and economical option for everyday cars, trucks, and SUVs. [Enclosed Car Shipping](/services/enclosed-auto-transport/) offers covered protection from weather and highway debris for classic, luxury, exotic, or collector vehicles. 

### Will the carrier pick up from my exact address?

+

The assigned motor carrier aims to pick up and deliver as close to your specified addresses as safe and legal truck access allows. Narrow residential streets or low-hanging trees may require meeting at a nearby parking lot. Read full details on our [Door-to-Door Car Shipping](/services/door-to-door-car-shipping/) page.
