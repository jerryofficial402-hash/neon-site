How Live Oak Car Shipping Works

  1. Request a quote: Tell us your pickup and delivery ZIP codes, vehicle details, preferred dates, and open or enclosed transport.
  2. Carrier matching: Once you book, we work to match your shipment with an available, qualified motor carrier for the route.
  3. Pickup and inspection: The driver documents your vehicle's condition on the Bill of Lading (BOL) before loading. Be present or have an authorized representative there to sign.
  4. Transit: Your vehicle travels safely to its destination. That carrier carries cargo insurance during transport; ask for current documentation for higher-value vehicles.
  5. Delivery: Inspect the vehicle against the pickup Bill of Lading before signing final delivery paperwork.

Frequently Asked Questions — Car Shipping in Live Oak, CA

How much does it cost to ship a car from Live Oak, CA?

Most car shipping quotes in 2026 fall between $0.50–$1.20 per mile for open transport, with typical shipments averaging $700–$1,200 depending on distance, vehicle size, and season. Enclosed transport runs roughly 30–50% higher. Get an exact quote for your Live Oak route using our free calculator.

How long does it take to ship a car from Live Oak?

Short regional routes (Sacramento, Chico, the Bay Area) typically take 1–3 days. Cross-country shipments average 7–10 days depending on carrier availability and route.

Is Neon Auto Transport licensed?

Yes. Neon Auto Transport is an auto transport broker authorized to arrange vehicle transportation through independent motor carriers (MC #1703787 and USDOT #4355879).

What's the difference between open and enclosed car shipping?

Open transport carries vehicles on a multi-car trailer exposed to the elements — it's the standard, most affordable option. Enclosed transport carries vehicles in a covered trailer, protecting them from weather and road debris, and is recommended for classic, luxury, or exotic vehicles.

Do I need to be present for pickup or delivery in Live Oak?

You or an authorized representative should be present at pickup and delivery to complete a vehicle condition inspection and sign the bill of lading.

Can I ship a vehicle that doesn't run?

Yes. Non-running vehicles can usually be transported, but the carrier needs to know in advance because special loading equipment may be required. Tell us the vehicle is inoperable when requesting your quote so we can match the shipment appropriately.

Can I ship from Live Oak to another state?

Yes. Neon Auto Transport coordinates vehicle shipping from Live Oak to all 50 states, subject to carrier availability and route requirements.
