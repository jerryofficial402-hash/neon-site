## Cost Calculator FAQs

How is my auto shipping quote calculated? +

Your quote is calculated using live market data from our nationwide carrier network. We factor in mileage, vehicle size, carrier type (open or enclosed), pickup and delivery locations, and current demand on your route to provide a realistic car shipping cost range before you book. 

Are there any hidden fees or upfront deposits required? +

No. Neon Auto Transport does not charge hidden fees or mandatory upfront deposits. The price you see in your quote already includes standard carrier, fuel, and insurance costs, so your auto transport pricing stays 100% transparent from start to finish. 

What is the difference between open and enclosed auto transport? +

Open transport is the most popular and affordable option, ideal for everyday vehicles and most relocations. Your car travels on an open trailer with other vehicles. Enclosed transport provides fully covered, weather-shielded protection, which is perfect for classic, exotic, and luxury vehicles that need extra care. 

How far in advance should I book my vehicle shipment? +

For most routes, booking 3–7 days before your desired pickup date works well. During busy seasons or for remote locations, booking earlier helps us secure the best carrier and lock in your car shipping rate.
