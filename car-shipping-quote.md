COST FACTORS

## What Affects Your Car Shipping Quote?

These core factors determine the price you see when you request an auto transport quote:

### Route Distance & Lane Popularity

Longer routes such as New York to Florida or Texas to California often have lower per-mile costs, while short regional moves may be more expensive per mile due to loading and scheduling time. 

### Vehicle Type & Condition

Sedans and compact cars use standard carrier space. SUVs, trucks, vans, and oversized vehicles require more room and increase your quote. Inoperable vehicles require winch service and special handling. 

### Carrier Type: Open vs Enclosed

Open car shipping quotes are ideal for everyday vehicles and budget-friendly moves. Enclosed car shipping quotes include full weather-shielded protection and zero exposure to road debris. For more details on carrier types, visit our [vehicle transport services](/services/) page. 

### Pickup & Delivery Locations

Quotes to and from major metro areas (e.g., Los Angeles, Dallas, Chicago, Miami) benefit from greater carrier availability. Rural or remote locations may require extra mileage or terminal coordination. 

### Seasonality & Timing

Demand spikes during summer moves, college relocations, and snowbird season, which can affect pricing on popular corridors.
