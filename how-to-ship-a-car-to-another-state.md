## When Should You Ship Your Car?

Shipping a vehicle may be practical when: 

  * You are moving several states away.
  * Driving would add substantial mileage and wear.
  * You are relocating more than one vehicle.
  * You have a second vehicle that another household member cannot drive.
  * You are moving for work, school, retirement, or military service.
  * You are buying or selling a vehicle across state lines.
  * You need to move a classic, luxury, or inoperable vehicle.
  * Your moving truck cannot accommodate the vehicle.
  * You need to arrive before the vehicle is available to drive.



Driving may still be appropriate for shorter relocations or when you need the vehicle immediately during the journey. Compare fuel, hotels, meals, tolls, lost time, mileage, and vehicle wear with the cost of professional transport.

## How Much Does It Cost to Ship a Car to Another State?

There is no single price for interstate car shipping. The quote depends on your vehicle, route, equipment, timing, and delivery conditions. 

### Main cost factors

  * **Distance:** Longer routes generally cost more overall.
  * **Vehicle size:** Trucks, vans, SUVs, and oversized vehicles may require more trailer space.
  * **Vehicle condition:** Inoperable vehicles may require winch equipment and extra labor.
  * **Transport type:** Open transport is usually more available and often less expensive than enclosed transport.
  * **Route density:** Major corridors may have more carrier availability than rural routes.
  * **Season:** Demand may increase during summer, holidays, military moves, and snowbird seasons.
  * **Pickup flexibility:** A date range may offer more options than a fixed appointment.
  * **Location access:** Narrow roads, private roads, gated areas, low bridges, or facility restrictions may require an alternate meeting point.
  * **Expedited service:** A short pickup window generally increases the cost.
  * **Fuel and market conditions:** Carrier costs and available trailer space can change.



### How to receive a more accurate quote

Provide pickup ZIP code, delivery ZIP code, vehicle year/make/model, dimensions (if modified), operable status, transport preference, earliest pickup date, required delivery timing, access limitations, and special equipment needs. 

Do not describe a non-running vehicle as an ordinary operable vehicle. Incomplete information can result in an inaccurate quote, delayed dispatch, or additional charges.

## Broker vs. Carrier for an Interstate Move

**🏢 What an Auto Transport Broker Does** A broker helps arrange transportation by collecting shipment info, coordinating quotes, searching carrier capacity, arranging pickup/delivery, communicating details, and supporting logistics. A broker does not operate the truck ([FMCSA Glossary](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/protect-your-move/glossary)). 

**🚛 What an Auto Transport Carrier Does** The motor carrier physically performs the shipment. The driver arrives at pickup, inspects the vehicle, records condition, loads and secures the vehicle, transports it, performs delivery inspection, and completes the bill of lading. 

### Should you use a broker or direct carrier?

Neither option is automatically better. A broker may be useful when you want route comparison, flexible dates, help finding carrier capacity, or specialty transport. A direct carrier may be useful when the company regularly serves your route and offers direct driver communication. Regardless of provider type, verify the company and understand who will physically transport your vehicle. 

## How to Choose a Car Shipping Company for Moving

**1\. Identify your shipment requirements** Determine origin, destination, vehicle type, operable status, open or enclosed preference, pickup window, delivery access, required arrival timing, and special equipment needs. 

**2\. Compare equivalent quotes** Request quotes using the same information. Compare total price, transport method, pickup window, transit estimate, payment terms, cancellation terms, and carrier-assignment process. 

**3\. Verify the company** Check legal business name, MC number, USDOT number, FMCSA authority, safety information, and insurance details via official [FMCSA Safety Records](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/safety/company-safety-records). 

**4\. Confirm the assigned carrier** If a broker arranges the shipment, ask when the carrier will be assigned, legal business name, USDOT/MC numbers, insurance details, equipment type, and driver contact procedures. 

**5\. Confirm access** Ask whether narrow streets, low branches, weight restrictions, gated communities, or private roads require an alternate meeting point near a commercial parking area. 

## Preparing, Shipping, and Receiving Your Car

### Before Pickup Preparation Checklist

  * Wash vehicle so existing damage is visible.
  * Remove personal belongings and toll transponders.
  * Photograph exterior from multiple angles.
  * Photograph existing scratches, dents, chips, and cracks.
  * Record current odometer mileage.
  * Check tire pressure and confirm battery is secure.
  * Disclose fluid leaks or mechanical quirks.
  * Keep fuel level low (approx. 1/4 tank) to limit weight.
  * Provide spare keys if required by carrier.
  * Ensure vehicle is accessible at agreed time.



**🚚 Pickup: What to Expect** Meet the driver, inspect vehicle together, compare condition with photos, record existing damage on bill of lading, confirm mileage/fuel, verify tie-down security, and retain a copy of paperwork. 

**🛣️ Transit: What to Expect** Transit depends on distance, traffic, weather, scheduling, and stops. Understand that pickup window spans multiple days; transit begins only after vehicle collection. 

**📦 Delivery: Final Inspection** Inspect vehicle before accepting, compare with pickup condition report, check all sides/glass/lights, start engine, photograph new damage, and write details on bill of lading before signing.

## Questions to Ask & Common Mistakes

### 20 Questions to Ask Before Booking

  1. Are you a broker, carrier, or marketplace?
  2. What is your legal business name?
  3. What is your MC or USDOT number?
  4. Which company will physically transport the vehicle?
  5. When will the carrier be assigned?
  6. What transport equipment will be used?
  7. Is the quote estimated or binding?
  8. What could cause the price to change?
  9. What is the pickup window?
  10. How long is estimated transit after pickup?
  11. Can the carrier access both addresses?
  12. What insurance information applies?
  13. What exclusions should I understand?
  14. What happens if the vehicle is inoperable?
  15. Are personal belongings permitted?
  16. What is the cancellation policy?
  17. Who handles damage claims?
  18. What document records vehicle condition?
  19. What payment is due at booking, pickup, or delivery?
  20. How are delays communicated?



### Common Mistakes to Avoid

  * **Choosing lowest quote automatically:** May reflect unachievable carrier rates.
  * **Hiding vehicle problems:** Inoperable/modified vehicles cause loading delays.
  * **Assuming exact address delivery:** Large trucks require accessible meeting points.
  * **Confusing pickup with transit time:** Pickup window is separate from transit days.
  * **Leaving personal items without permission:** Belongings may be excluded from coverage.
  * **Failing to document condition:** Photos and completed bill of lading are required.
  * **Booking too late:** Last-minute moves have limited equipment options.
  * **Relying on one review platform:** Combine reviews with FMCSA regulatory checks.



## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I ship my car to another state?

Request quotes using your pickup and delivery ZIP codes, vehicle details, condition, transport method, and preferred dates. Verify the provider, confirm the assigned carrier, prepare the vehicle, complete pickup documentation, and inspect it at delivery. 

### Is it cheaper to drive or ship a car to another state?

It depends on distance, fuel prices, hotels, meals, tolls, time away from work, mileage, and vehicle wear. Compare the complete cost of driving with a route-specific auto transport quote. 

### How much does it cost to ship a car to another state?

The price depends on distance, vehicle size, condition, transport type, route demand, season, locations, fuel costs, and timing. Request a shipment-specific quote rather than relying on a universal price. 

### What is the cheapest way to ship a car interstate?

Open transport is often the most economical option for an operable standard vehicle. Flexible pickup dates and accessible pickup and delivery locations may also increase carrier availability, but the lowest quote is not always the best value. 

### Is open or enclosed transport better for moving?

Open transport is often practical for ordinary cars, SUVs, and trucks. Enclosed transport may be preferable for classic, luxury, exotic, collector, or customized vehicles requiring greater protection from weather and road debris. 

### How long does it take to ship a car to another state?

The timeframe depends on route distance, traffic, weather, carrier scheduling, and pickup and delivery conditions. Ask for a pickup window and a separate transit estimate. 

### Can I ship an inoperable car to another state?

Often, yes. Disclose the condition before booking because the carrier may need a winch or specialized loading equipment. 

### Can I ship a car door-to-door?

Usually, pickup and delivery can occur as close as safely and legally possible to the requested addresses. Large trucks may require a nearby accessible meeting point. 

### Should I use a broker or direct carrier?

A broker may provide carrier coordination and route options, while a direct carrier may provide direct communication with the hauling company. Verify the company’s role, authority, insurance information, and written terms either way. 

### Are auto transport companies insured?

The assigned motor carrier should provide applicable insurance information. Ask about coverage limits, exclusions, deductibles, and the claims process. Do not assume that a broker’s authority or bond is the same as the carrier’s cargo coverage. 

### Can I leave items inside my car during transport?

Only if the provider and carrier permit it in writing. Personal property may be excluded from cargo coverage or prohibited by the carrier. 

### What is a bill of lading?

A bill of lading is the shipment document and condition report used at pickup and delivery. It records vehicle details, mileage, transport terms, and visible damage. 

### How far in advance should I schedule car transport?

Book as early as practical, especially during summer, holidays, military relocation periods, and snowbird seasons. Earlier planning usually gives you more scheduling flexibility.
