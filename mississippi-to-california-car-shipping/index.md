## How a car gets from Mississippi to California

From central Mississippi, carriers usually head west on I-20 through Shreveport to Dallas, then take I-20 and I-10, or I-40, to California. From the Gulf Coast, I-10 runs almost the whole way, through Houston, San Antonio and Phoenix.

Common reasons include military moves from Keesler AFB or Columbus AFB to California bases, job relocations, and cars bought from California sellers or dealers.

### Before you book

- Pickups near I-20, I-55 or I-10 are the fastest to schedule. Rural Mississippi may need a meeting point in town.
- Summer heat along I-10 is hard on weak batteries. Have yours checked before a long trip.
- For California registration, plan for a smog check once the car arrives.
