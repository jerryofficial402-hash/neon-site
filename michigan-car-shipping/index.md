## Tips & Tricks for Saving on Vehicle Shipping

### Off Season Savings

Car shipping rates drop during the off-season. Even shifting your shipment by a few weeks can lead to real savings. Avoid peak moving seasons like summer and major holidays to get the best rates.

### Flexible Pickup & Delivery

Choosing a five-day window for pickup and delivery can lower your shipping cost. Carriers can plan routes more easily with flexible dates, which means more options and better pricing for you.

### Hub City Advantages

For pickups and drop-offs in Michigan, choose a location near major highways. Spots close to I-75 or near Detroit attract more carriers, giving you more options and more competitive rates on your shipment.

### Open Trailers

Open trailers cost less than enclosed trailers and work well for most standard vehicles. If your car doesn’t need extra protection from the elements, an open trailer is the smart, budget-friendly choice.

## Michigan Vehicle Transport FAQs

How do I prepare my vehicle for transit to/from MI? +

Remove personal belongings and take clear photos of your vehicle's condition. Due to Michigan's harsh winters and road salt, verify your antifreeze levels. Keep the gas tank at a quarter full, disable alarms, and confirm the car starts easily in cold weather. 

Is my vehicle insured transporting from Michigan? +

Yes, your vehicle is fully insured during transit. Carriers traveling the Great Lakes State along I-75 and I-94 are legally required to carry active cargo insurance to cover any damages incurred. 

How far out should I schedule my pickup service in Michigan? +

We suggest booking 1–2 weeks in advance for most Michigan transports. During the intense winter snowbird migration to Florida or Arizona, booking 2–4 weeks ahead helps lock in the best carriers and rates. 

How do I pay for my auto transport? +

No payment is due upfront. You provide a payment method to secure your carrier. The balance can be paid securely by credit card, or you can pay the remaining balance to the driver with cash or a certified check on delivery day. 

Can I pack personal items inside my vehicle? +

You can pack up to 100 lbs of personal belongings in the trunk or below the window line. Remember that carriers are not responsible for personal items, so keep all valuables out of the vehicle. 

Do I need to be present at both pickup and delivery? +

Yes, you or an authorized adult must be present at pickup and delivery in Michigan. This ensures proper inspection and signing of the Bill of Lading, which serves as your official vehicle condition receipt. 

What's the difference between Open vs Enclosed Auto Transport? +

Open transport is the most popular and affordable method, though your car will be exposed to Michigan's weather. Enclosed transport provides a fully covered trailer, shielding your vehicle from snow and road salt, ideal for luxury and classic cars. 

## Factors that Affect Michigan Shipping Prices

### Distance

The farther your car travels, the more you’ll pay. However, longer trips often cost less per mile than shorter ones.

### Route

Shipping along major highways is cheaper and faster. Rural or off-the-beaten-path routes cost more due to limited carrier access.

### Season

Summer and snowbird season drive prices up. Shipping during slower months, like fall or early spring, can save you money.

### Vehicle Type

Larger vehicles like SUVs and trucks take up more space and use more fuel. Expect to pay more than you would for a standard sedan.

### Traffic Patterns

Busy travel weeks, like Thanksgiving, can slow down delivery times. Booking during lighter traffic windows helps keep costs and delays down.

### Carrier Availability

During peak season, carriers fill up fast. You may wait longer for pickup, so book early to lock in your preferred dates.

### Open vs Enclosed

Open trailers are the most affordable option. Enclosed trailers offer extra protection but cost more — ideal for luxury or classic cars.

### Population Density

Shipping to or from a busy city can cost more. Dense urban areas have more traffic and tighter access for large carriers.
