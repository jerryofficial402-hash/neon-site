1. 100% Vetted & Insured Carrier Network

Neon Auto Transport LLC operates as a licensed auto transport broker. We do not dispatch your vehicle to random truckers. Every motor carrier assigned to your transport undergoes rigorous compliance checks:

  • Verification of active USDOT authority and FMCSA licensing.
  • Active cargo insurance coverage verification ($100,000 to $250,000+).
  • Minimum 98% driver safety and historical on-time delivery record.
