Why Choose Neon Auto Transport?

Neon Auto Transport helps customers arrange door-to-door vehicle shipping across the United States. As a licensed auto transport broker, we match your shipment with an independent motor carrier based on your route, vehicle, transport preferences, timing, and carrier availability.

Before you book, we explain the transport options, provide written shipment details, and help you understand the pickup window, pricing terms, carrier assignment process, and inspection steps.

Broker Transparency

Neon Auto Transport LLC is a licensed auto transport broker operating under MC #1703787 and USDOT #4355879. We arrange transportation through independently owned motor carriers. The assigned motor carrier—not Neon Auto Transport—physically picks up, transports, and delivers the vehicle.

You can verify a broker’s or carrier’s authority through the FMCSA Licensing and Insurance system before booking.

What You Can Expect

Clear Quote Information

Your quote is based on the route, vehicle, pickup and delivery access, transport type, requested timing, and current carrier availability. Before booking, ask us how the quoted amount, payment schedule, pickup window, and any applicable terms work for your shipment.

Transport Options That Fit Your Vehicle

Choose open transport for the most common and typically more economical shipping option, or enclosed transport for vehicles that need added protection from weather and road exposure. We can also discuss expedited scheduling when timing is a priority.

Carrier Assignment Coordination

After you approve the booking details, we seek a carrier that fits your route and shipment requirements. Carrier availability and scheduling can change, so pickup and delivery are provided as estimated windows rather than exact guarantees unless expressly confirmed in writing.

Condition Inspection Documentation

At pickup and delivery, the carrier typically completes a vehicle condition inspection on the Bill of Lading. Review the report, note any concerns before signing, and keep a copy for your records.

Questions to Ask Any Auto Transport Company

Before booking with any auto transport provider, ask:

  • Are you a broker or a motor carrier?
  • What are your MC and USDOT numbers?
  • Is my quote an estimate or a binding price under stated conditions?
  • What payment, cancellation, and refund terms apply?
  • Which carrier will physically transport my vehicle?
  • How can I review insurance information for the assigned carrier?
  • What should I do if my street cannot accommodate a large transport truck?
  • How do pickup and delivery inspections work?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Neon Auto Transport a broker or a carrier?

Neon Auto Transport is a licensed auto transport broker. We arrange vehicle shipments with independently owned motor carriers that physically transport the vehicle.

Can I verify Neon Auto Transport’s authority?

Yes. You can search MC #1703787 and USDOT #4355879 through FMCSA’s Licensing and Insurance system.

Who completes the vehicle inspection?

The assigned carrier typically completes the Bill of Lading condition inspection with you or your authorized representative at pickup and delivery. Review the document carefully and retain a copy.

Will the driver pick up directly at my address?

The carrier will aim to pick up and deliver as close as safely and legally possible. Narrow streets, gated communities, low-clearance areas, parking restrictions, or local rules may require a nearby meeting location.

Can I choose open or enclosed transport?

Yes. Open transport is the common choice for everyday vehicles. Enclosed shipping provides additional protection for vehicles that need it.
