## Tips & Tricks for Saving on Vehicle Shipping

### Off Season Savings

Car shipping rates drop during the off-season. Even shifting your shipment by a few weeks can lead to real savings. Avoid peak moving seasons like summer and major holidays to get the best rates.

### Flexible Pickup & Delivery

Choosing a five-day window for pickup and delivery can lower your shipping cost. Carriers can plan routes more easily with flexible dates, which means more options and better pricing for you.

### Hub City Advantages

For pickups and drop-offs in Maryland, choose a location near major highways. Spots close to I-95 or near Baltimore attract more carriers, giving you more options and more competitive rates on your shipment.

### Open Trailers

Open trailers cost less than enclosed trailers and work well for most standard vehicles. If your car doesn’t need extra protection from the elements, an open trailer is the smart, budget-friendly choice.

## Maryland Vehicle Transport FAQs

How do I prepare my vehicle for transit to/from MD? +

Clear out any personal belongings and take photos of existing damage. Maryland experiences diverse weather from coastal storms to winter snow, so check your fluid levels and tire pressure. Keep the gas tank at a quarter full and disable any alarms. 

Is my vehicle insured transporting from Maryland? +

Yes, your vehicle is fully insured during transit. Carriers operating in the Free State along major routes like I-95 and I-70 are required to hold active cargo insurance to cover any potential transport damage. 

How far out should I schedule my pickup service in Maryland? +

For shipments in or out of Maryland, we recommend booking 1–2 weeks in advance. During busy summer moving months or snowbird seasons, scheduling 2–4 weeks ahead helps secure your preferred carrier and dates. 

How do I pay for my auto transport? +

No upfront payment is required at the time of booking. Provide a payment method to lock in your carrier. You can then pay the balance via card, or directly to the driver with cash or a certified check upon delivery. 

Can I pack personal items inside my vehicle? +

You may include up to 100 lbs of personal items, stored securely in the trunk or below the window line. Carriers are not liable for personal items, so be sure to remove any valuables before transport. 

Do I need to be present at both pickup and delivery? +

Yes, you or a trusted adult must be present at both locations in Maryland to inspect the vehicle alongside the driver and sign the Bill of Lading, which acts as the official receipt of the car's condition. 

What's the difference between Open vs Enclosed Auto Transport? +

Open transport is the most economical choice but exposes your vehicle to Maryland's weather. Enclosed transport uses a fully covered trailer to shield your car from elements and road debris, making it the ideal choice for luxury or classic vehicles. 

## Factors that Affect Maryland Shipping Prices

### Distance

The farther your car travels, the more you’ll pay. However, longer trips often cost less per mile than shorter ones.

### Route

Shipping along major highways is cheaper and faster. Rural or off-the-beaten-path routes cost more due to limited carrier access.

### Season

Summer and snowbird season drive prices up. Shipping during slower months, like fall or early spring, can save you money.

### Vehicle Type

Larger vehicles like SUVs and trucks take up more space and use more fuel. Expect to pay more than you would for a standard sedan.

### Traffic Patterns

Busy travel weeks, like Thanksgiving, can slow down delivery times. Booking during lighter traffic windows helps keep costs and delays down.

### Carrier Availability

During peak season, carriers fill up fast. You may wait longer for pickup, so book early to lock in your preferred dates.

### Open vs Enclosed

Open trailers are the most affordable option. Enclosed trailers offer extra protection but cost more — ideal for luxury or classic cars.

### Population Density

Shipping to or from a busy city can cost more. Dense urban areas have more traffic and tighter access for large carriers.
