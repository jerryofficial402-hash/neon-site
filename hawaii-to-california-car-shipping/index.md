## How a car gets from Hawaii to California

Cars leave Hawaii by ship. Most sail from Honolulu, and the other islands connect through their own ports. They arrive at a California port, usually in Southern California or the Bay Area. From there, a carrier truck can take the car to your door anywhere in the state.

Most Hawaii-to-California shipments are military families finishing a tour on Oahu, people moving back to the mainland, and residents selling or relocating a car.

### Before you book

- Ocean carriers usually require an empty car, a low fuel level, and current registration and title or lienholder paperwork. We share the exact rules for your sailing.
- Military families should check what their PCS orders cover. One car is often shipped at government expense.
- If you need the car delivered inland, we book the truck from the port as soon as the ship's arrival date is set.
