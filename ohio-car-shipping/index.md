## Tips & Tricks for Saving on Vehicle Shipping

### Off Season Savings

Car shipping rates drop during the off-season. Even shifting your shipment by a few weeks can lead to real savings. Avoid peak moving seasons like summer and major holidays to get the best rates.

### Flexible Pickup & Delivery

Choosing a five-day window for pickup and delivery can lower your shipping cost. Carriers can plan routes more easily with flexible dates, which means more options and better pricing for you.

### Hub City Advantages

For pickups and drop-offs in Ohio, choose a location near major highways. Spots close to I-71 or near Columbus attract more carriers, giving you more options and more competitive rates on your shipment.

### Open Trailers

Open trailers cost less than enclosed trailers and work well for most standard vehicles. If your car doesn’t need extra protection from the elements, an open trailer is the smart, budget-friendly choice.

## Ohio Vehicle Transport FAQs

How do I prepare my vehicle for transit to/from OH? +

When shipping to or from Ohio, remove all personal belongings and document any existing damage with photos. Make sure your gas tank is only a quarter full, disable alarms, and verify the vehicle is operational. 

Is my vehicle insured transporting from Ohio? +

Yes, your vehicle is fully insured during transport. Every carrier operating on Ohio routes like I-71 and I-70 must carry active cargo insurance to cover any potential transit damage. 

How far out should I schedule my pickup service in Ohio? +

For Ohio shipments, we recommend booking 1–2 weeks in advance. During harsh Midwestern winters or busy summer moves, booking 2–4 weeks ahead helps ensure preferred dates. 

How do I pay for my auto transport? +

No upfront payment is required at booking. Provide a payment method to secure your Ohio carrier, and pay the remaining balance to the driver upon delivery with cash or a certified check. 

Can I pack personal items inside my vehicle? +

Yes, you can pack up to 100 lbs of personal items safely stowed in the trunk or below the window line. Remember that carriers crossing state lines out of OH aren't liable for these items. 

Do I need to be present at both pickup and delivery? +

Yes, it is important that you or a trusted adult is present for pickup and delivery in Ohio to inspect the vehicle and sign the Bill of Lading. 

What's the difference between Open vs Enclosed Auto Transport? +

Open transport is the most economical and common choice for Ohio shipments. Enclosed transport shields your vehicle from Ohio's lake-effect snow and road salt in a covered trailer, making it ideal for luxury or classic cars, but it costs more. 

## Factors that Affect Ohio Shipping Prices

### Distance

The farther your car travels, the more you’ll pay. However, longer trips often cost less per mile than shorter ones.

### Route

Shipping along major highways is cheaper and faster. Rural or off-the-beaten-path routes cost more due to limited carrier access.

### Season

Summer and snowbird season drive prices up. Shipping during slower months, like fall or early spring, can save you money.

### Vehicle Type

Larger vehicles like SUVs and trucks take up more space and use more fuel. Expect to pay more than you would for a standard sedan.

### Traffic Patterns

Busy travel weeks, like Thanksgiving, can slow down delivery times. Booking during lighter traffic windows helps keep costs and delays down.

### Carrier Availability

During peak season, carriers fill up fast. You may wait longer for pickup, so book early to lock in your preferred dates.

### Open vs Enclosed

Open trailers are the most affordable option. Enclosed trailers offer extra protection but cost more — ideal for luxury or classic cars.

### Population Density

Shipping to or from a busy city can cost more. Dense urban areas have more traffic and tighter access for large carriers.
