Little Rock Auto Transport

Little Rock sits at the crossing point of I-20, I-30, I-35E, and I-45, which makes it one of the best-connected cities in the country for auto transport. Carriers running north-south between Oklahoma and the Gulf Coast, or east-west between the Southeast and the Southwest, pass directly through Little Rock — which keeps car shipping Little Rock TX quotes on the more competitive end and pickup windows short. Little Rock auto transport covers the full metro, from downtown out to Plano, Irving, Frisco, and Richardson.

Little Rock Car Shipping FAQ

How much does it cost to ship a car from Little Rock? +
Little Rock car shipping runs roughly $350–$550 for Arkansas Triangle routes and $650–$1,000 for cross-country hauls to the Midwest or East Coast on an open carrier.
What’s the fastest way to ship a car from Little Rock to Houston? +
Little Rock to Houston is one of the busiest lanes in the state and typically ships within a single day of booking on an open carrier, given how much carrier traffic already runs that corridor.
Does weather affect car shipping in Little Rock? +
Spring hail (March– May) is the main seasonal factor in North Arkansas. Many Little Rock shippers choose enclosed transport during that window to protect a higher-value vehicle’s paint and glass.
