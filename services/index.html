How to Choose a Vehicle Transport Option

Choose open auto transport for the most common and typically more economical option for standard vehicles. Choose enclosed car shipping when added protection from weather and road exposure is important. Consider expedited transport when your pickup window is limited.

Before booking, compare written quote details—not only the advertised price. Confirm whether you are working with a broker or carrier, review the pickup window, ask about payment and cancellation terms, and understand how the assigned carrier and vehicle inspection process will work.

Vehicle Transport Services FAQs

What types of vehicles can be shipped?

Neon Auto Transport can help arrange transportation for many cars, SUVs, pickup trucks, motorcycles, classic vehicles, dealer units, and fleet vehicles. Share the vehicle year, make, model, condition, and any modifications so appropriate transport options can be reviewed.

Is Neon Auto Transport a broker or a carrier?

Neon Auto Transport is a licensed auto transport broker. We arrange shipments through independently owned motor carriers that physically transport the vehicle.

Is open or enclosed transport better?

Open transport is the most common option and is generally suitable for everyday vehicles. Enclosed transport provides added protection from weather and road exposure and is often chosen for classic, luxury, exotic, and collector vehicles.

Will the carrier pick up from my exact address?

The assigned carrier aims to pick up and deliver as close to your selected addresses as safely and legally possible. Truck-access restrictions may require a nearby meeting point.

How do I get a vehicle transport quote?

Use the quote form or car shipping cost calculator, or call with your pickup location, delivery location, vehicle details, preferred dates, and transport preference.

Need to move multiple vehicles or coordinate a business relocation?

Tell us the vehicle count, pickup and delivery locations, preferred dates, and transport requirements. We will review available carrier options and provide a written quote for your shipment.
