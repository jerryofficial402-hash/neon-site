TIPS & TRICKS

## Tips & Tricks for Saving on Vehicle Shipping

### Pro Tip for Luxury / Exotic Car Shipping Services

Always opt for enclosed transport to protect against road debris and weather.

### Flexible Pickup & Delivery

Choosing a five-day window for pickup and delivery can lower your shipping cost. Carriers can plan routes more easily with flexible dates, which means more options and better pricing for you.

### Hub City Advantages

For your auto transport needs, choose a location near major highways. Spots close to major interstates attract more carriers, giving you more options and more competitive rates on your shipment.

### Open Trailers

Open trailers cost less than enclosed trailers and work well for most standard vehicles. If your car doesn’t need extra protection from the elements, an open trailer is the smart, budget-friendly choice.

## Factors that Affect Luxury / Exotic Car Shipping Services Costs

### Distance

The farther your car travels, the more you’ll pay. However, longer trips often cost less per mile than shorter ones.

### Route

Shipping along major highways is cheaper and faster. Rural or off-the-beaten-path routes cost more due to limited carrier access.

### Season

Summer and snowbird season drive prices up. Shipping during slower months, like fall or early spring, can save you money.

### Vehicle Type

Larger vehicles like SUVs and trucks take up more space and use more fuel. Expect to pay more than you would for a standard sedan.

### Traffic Patterns

Busy travel weeks, like Thanksgiving, can slow down delivery times. Booking during lighter traffic windows helps keep costs and delays down.

### Carrier Availability

During peak season, carriers fill up fast. You may wait longer for pickup, so book early to lock in your preferred dates.

### Open vs Enclosed

Open trailers are the most affordable option. Enclosed trailers offer extra protection but cost more — ideal for luxury or classic cars.

### Population Density

Shipping to or from a busy city can cost more. Dense urban areas have more traffic and tighter access for large carriers.

## Luxury Car Shipping FAQs

How are luxury and exotic cars protected during enclosed transport? +

Luxury vehicles are loaded via hydraulic lift gate onto soft-surface enclosed trailers. They are secured with soft straps that never touch the paint, and wheel chocks protect low-profile tires. The enclosed trailer blocks all weather, debris, and UV exposure. 

Can I ship a Ferrari, Lamborghini, or Rolls-Royce with Neon? +

Yes. We regularly ship exotic and ultra-luxury vehicles including Ferraris, Lamborghinis, McLarens, Rolls-Royces, and Bentleys. Our drivers are experienced with low-clearance supercars and high-value collector vehicles. 

Do you offer single-car dedicated transport for exotic vehicles? +

Yes, we offer single-car dedicated enclosed transport for maximum security. Your vehicle is the only one on the trailer, eliminating any risk of contact with other vehicles. This premium service costs 50-100% more than shared enclosed transport. 

What insurance coverage is provided for high-value vehicles? +

All carriers carry a minimum of $250,000 in cargo insurance. For vehicles valued over $250,000, we can arrange supplemental insurance to cover the full declared value. Discuss coverage options with your shipping coordinator before booking. 

How far in advance should I book luxury car shipping? +

We recommend booking 2-4 weeks in advance for enclosed luxury transport. Single-car dedicated carriers require 3-6 weeks notice. Early booking ensures we match you with an experienced exotic car specialist driver.
