## What Is Snowbird Car Shipping?

Snowbird car shipping is a specialized seasonal auto transport service designed for retirees, remote workers, and seasonal residents who relocate from cold northern states to southern and western sunbelt states for the winter months.

Rather than spending 20 to 40 exhausting hours behind the wheel on congested interstate highways (like I-95, I-75, or I-10) during unpredictable early-winter weather, snowbirds fly directly to their winter destination while an FMCSA-authorized carrier transports their vehicle safely to their front door.

## When Should Snowbirds Ship Their Cars?

* **Southbound (October–November):** Book 3–4 weeks ahead. Carrier capacity on NY→FL, Midwest→FL, and Midwest→AZ lanes tightens sharply after mid-November, with December carrying peak-season demand.
* **Northbound (March–April):** The return rush mirrors the fall migration. The weeks right after Easter are typically the quietest and most cost-effective window for northbound moves.
* **Year-Round Continuity:** Recurring snowbird customers who coordinate both legs with Neon benefit from seamless carrier coordination and reliable seasonal scheduling year after year.

## Factors that Affect Snowbird Car Shipping Costs

1. **Route Distance:** Longer journeys cost more overall in fuel and driver hours, though per-mile rates drop on routes over 1,500 miles.
2. **Corridor Accessibility:** Major interstates (I-95, I-75, I-10) have daily carrier traffic. Off-route rural pickups may require flexible meeting points.
3. **Peak Seasonal Demand:** Fall southbound and spring northbound migrations create tight carrier capacity. Booking early locks in carrier rates.
4. **Vehicle Size & Weight:** Larger SUVs and trucks take more trailer footprint and payload than compact sedans.
5. **Trailer Type:** Open transport is the standard budget-friendly method; enclosed trailers protect luxury and collector vehicles.
6. **Lead Time:** 2 to 4 weeks lead time allows dispatchers to secure preferred carrier routes without expedited premiums.

## How to Prepare Your Car for Seasonal Shipping

1. **Wash & Inspect Vehicle:** Clean vehicle before pickup so condition can be accurately noted on the Bill of Lading.
2. **Maintain 1/4 Tank of Fuel:** Keeps trailer weight optimal while providing fuel for loading and unloading.
3. **Remove Personal Valuables:** Take out toll passes, parking remotes, and personal valuables.
4. **Check Tires & Battery:** Ensure battery is charged and tires properly inflated for immediate arrival use.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is the best time to ship my car south for the winter?
Most snowbirds ship their cars south between October and November before winter hits. Book 3-4 weeks ahead during this peak period to secure preferred dates and avoid surge pricing that kicks in during late November and December.

### How much does snowbird car shipping cost between northern and southern states?
Typical snowbird routes (NY to FL, MI to AZ, IL to TX) cost $650-$1,500 depending on distance and vehicle type. Booking early (September-October) can save 15-20% compared to last-minute December bookings during peak demand.

### Should I ship my car both ways or buy a second car at my winter home?
For stays of 3+ months, shipping your own car both ways is usually more economical and comfortable. Round-trip shipping costs $1,600-$3,600, while buying and selling a second car involves taxes, insurance, registration, and depreciation.

### Can I get a discount for shipping my car north and south each year?
Many customers ship both directions with us each season. Booking both legs together lets us coordinate carrier capacity across the year — ask your transport specialist about round-trip scheduling when you request your quote.

### What are the most popular snowbird car shipping routes?
Top routes include New York to Florida, Michigan to Arizona, Illinois to Texas, Minnesota to California, and Massachusetts to South Carolina. We run dedicated carriers on these high-demand routes throughout the snowbird season.
