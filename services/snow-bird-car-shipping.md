## Factors that Affect Snow Bird Car Shipping Costs

### Distance

The farther your car travels, the more you’ll pay. However, longer trips often cost less per mile than shorter ones.

### Route

Shipping along major highways is cheaper and faster. Rural or off-the-beaten-path routes cost more due to limited carrier access.

### Season

Summer and snowbird season drive prices up. Shipping during slower months, like fall or early spring, can save you money.

### Vehicle Type

Larger vehicles like SUVs and trucks take up more space and use more fuel. Expect to pay more than you would for a standard sedan.

### Traffic Patterns

Busy travel weeks, like Thanksgiving, can slow down delivery times. Booking during lighter traffic windows helps keep costs and delays down.

### Carrier Availability

During peak season, carriers fill up fast. You may wait longer for pickup, so book early to lock in your preferred dates.

### Open vs Enclosed

Open trailers are the most affordable option. Enclosed trailers offer extra protection but cost more — ideal for luxury or classic cars.

### Population Density

Shipping to or from a busy city can cost more. Dense urban areas have more traffic and tighter access for large carriers.

Book at least 3-4 weeks early during the fall to beat the seasonal rush.

### Flexible Pickup & Delivery

Choosing a five-day window for pickup and delivery can lower your shipping cost. Carriers can plan routes more easily with flexible dates, which means more options and better pricing for you.

### Hub City Advantages

For your auto transport needs, choose a location near major highways. Spots close to major interstates attract more carriers, giving you more options and more competitive rates on your shipment.

### Open Trailers

Open trailers cost less than enclosed trailers and work well for most standard vehicles. If your car doesn’t need extra protection from the elements, an open trailer is the smart, budget-friendly choice.

## Snowbird Car Shipping FAQs

When is the best time to ship my car south for the winter? +

Most snowbirds ship their cars south between October and November before winter hits. Book 3-4 weeks ahead during this peak period to secure preferred dates and avoid surge pricing that kicks in during late November and December. 

How much does snowbird car shipping cost between northern and southern states? +

Typical snowbird routes (NY to FL, MI to AZ, IL to TX) cost $800-$1,800 depending on distance and vehicle type. Booking early (September-October) can save 15-20% compared to last-minute December bookings during peak demand. 

Should I ship my car both ways or buy a second car at my winter home? +

For stays of 3+ months, shipping your own car both ways is usually more economical and comfortable. Round-trip shipping costs $1,600-$3,600, while buying and selling a second car involves taxes, insurance, registration, and depreciation. 

Can I get a discount for shipping my car north and south each year? +

Yes, we offer snowbird round-trip discounts of 10-15% when you book both legs together. Recurring annual customers also qualify for loyalty pricing that locks in savings year after year. 

What are the most popular snowbird car shipping routes? +

Top routes include New York to Florida, Michigan to Arizona, Illinois to Texas, Minnesota to California, and Massachusetts to South Carolina. We run dedicated carriers on these high-demand routes throughout the snowbird season.
