Single vs. Multi-Motorcycle Trailer Shipping

Single-Motorcycle Trailer

Ideal for shipping one bike, a single-motorcycle trailer offers dedicated space and handling with no other vehicles sharing the load — a good fit for high-value, custom, or vintage motorcycles where minimal handling matters most.

Multi-Motorcycle Trailer

For shipping two or more motorcycles at once — common for dealers, collectors, or riders relocating with multiple bikes — a multi-motorcycle trailer carries several bikes securely in one shipment, often at a lower per-bike cost than shipping them separately.

How to Prepare Your Motorcycle for Shipping

  1. Wash the motorcycle so any existing scratches or dents are easy to document.
  2. Keep the fuel tank to about a quarter full.
  3. Disconnect or secure the battery if advised by your carrier.
  4. Remove or secure any loose accessories, saddlebags, or windshields.
  5. Check tire pressure — carriers typically require the bike to roll on/off under its own tire pressure.
  6. Take timestamped photos from all angles before pickup.

How much does it cost to ship a motorcycle? +
Motorcycle shipping typically costs between $300 and $800 depending on distance, motorcycle type, and whether you choose open or enclosed transport.
Can you ship a non-running motorcycle? +
Yes, non-running motorcycles can be shipped using specialized loading equipment. This may affect final pricing and delivery timeline.
Do I need to be present at pickup and delivery? +
Yes, either you or an authorized representative should be present at both pickup and delivery to inspect the motorcycle and sign the Bill of Lading and Proof of Delivery.
Is my motorcycle insured during transport? +
Yes. Every carrier in our network carries active cargo insurance, with full coverage details provided before pickup.
