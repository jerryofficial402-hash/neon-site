## Car Buyer Auto Transport FAQs

I just bought a car online — how do I get it shipped to me? +

Simply provide us with the pickup address (seller location) and your delivery address. We handle all coordination with the seller for pickup scheduling. You receive tracking updates throughout transit and the driver delivers directly to your door. 

Can you pick up a car from a private seller I found on Craigslist or Facebook? +

Yes. We coordinate directly with private sellers for pickup. Provide us the seller address and contact info, and we handle the rest. The seller just needs to be present to hand over the keys and sign the Bill of Lading at pickup. 

Should I inspect the car before the carrier picks it up? +

Absolutely. We strongly recommend a pre-purchase inspection before shipping. If you cannot inspect in person, request detailed photos and a video walkaround from the seller. Document any existing damage on the Bill of Lading at pickup. 

How do I pay for a car I am having shipped? +

Vehicle payment is handled directly between you and the seller — we only handle shipping payment. We recommend using secure payment methods and verifying title transfer before the carrier picks up the vehicle. 

What happens if the car I bought looks different when it arrives? +

The Bill of Lading signed at pickup documents the vehicle condition with photos. If damage occurs during transit, it is covered by the carrier cargo insurance. If the vehicle was misrepresented by the seller, that is a separate dispute to resolve with them.

## Tips & Tricks for Saving on Vehicle Shipping

### Pro Tip for Car Buyer Auto Transport

Ask the seller to take timestamped photos before the carrier arrives.

### Flexible Pickup & Delivery

Choosing a five-day window for pickup and delivery can lower your shipping cost. Carriers can plan routes more easily with flexible dates, which means more options and better pricing for you.

### Hub City Advantages

For your auto transport needs, choose a location near major highways. Spots close to major interstates attract more carriers, giving you more options and more competitive rates on your shipment.

### Open Trailers

Open trailers cost less than enclosed trailers and work well for most standard vehicles. If your car doesn’t need extra protection from the elements, an open trailer is the smart, budget-friendly choice.

## Factors that Affect Car Buyer Auto Transport Costs

### Distance

The farther your car travels, the more you’ll pay. However, longer trips often cost less per mile than shorter ones.

### Route

Shipping along major highways is cheaper and faster. Rural or off-the-beaten-path routes cost more due to limited carrier access.

### Season

Summer and snowbird season drive prices up. Shipping during slower months, like fall or early spring, can save you money.

### Vehicle Type

Larger vehicles like SUVs and trucks take up more space and use more fuel. Expect to pay more than you would for a standard sedan.

### Traffic Patterns

Busy travel weeks, like Thanksgiving, can slow down delivery times. Booking during lighter traffic windows helps keep costs and delays down.

### Carrier Availability

During peak season, carriers fill up fast. You may wait longer for pickup, so book early to lock in your preferred dates.

### Open vs Enclosed

Open trailers are the most affordable option. Enclosed trailers offer extra protection but cost more — ideal for luxury or classic cars.

### Population Density

Shipping to or from a busy city can cost more. Dense urban areas have more traffic and tighter access for large carriers.
