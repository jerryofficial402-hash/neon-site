How Do You Ship a Car to Another State?

To ship a car to another state, request a quote with your pickup location, destination, vehicle details, preferred dates, and transport type. After you review the available options and approve booking terms, a suitable carrier is assigned based on your route and vehicle requirements.

At pickup and delivery, you or an authorized representative reviews the vehicle condition with the carrier and signs the Bill of Lading. Door-to-door service means the carrier aims to meet you at or near your selected addresses, although restricted streets or access conditions may require a nearby meeting point.

How Much Does It Cost to Ship a Car to Another State?

The cost to ship a car to another state depends on your exact route, vehicle, transport type, pickup and delivery access, dates, and carrier availability. Short routes can have higher per-mile pricing because loading, scheduling, and operating costs do not decrease in direct proportion to mileage. Long-distance routes generally cost more overall but may have a lower per-mile estimate.

Factors that affect interstate car shipping cost:

  • Distance and demand on the route
  • Vehicle size, weight, condition, and modifications
  • Open or enclosed trailer selection
  • Rural, urban, gated-community, or restricted-access addresses
  • Flexible or time-sensitive pickup dates
  • Seasonal demand and weather conditions
  • Inoperable-vehicle or specialty-loading needs
  • Carrier availability at the time of dispatch

How Long Does It Take to Ship a Car to Another State?

Transit time depends on the route, total distance, carrier schedule, traffic, weather, required loading and unloading stops, and federal Hours-of-Service rules. Transit begins after pickup; the carrier-assignment and pickup window are separate from driving time.

As a general planning guide, regional state-to-state shipments may take several days, while long cross-country shipments can take approximately one to two weeks from pickup depending on conditions. Your coordinator provides the available pickup-window and estimated transit information for your specific route.

Open vs. Enclosed Interstate Car Shipping

Choosing between open and enclosed auto transport depends on your vehicle type, protection preference, and budget.

Open Auto Transport

Common choice for standard cars, SUVs, trucks, and relocations

Vehicles travel on an open multi-car trailer and are exposed to normal weather and road conditions.

Enclosed Car Shipping

Added protection for classic, luxury, exotic, or collector vehicles

Covered trailer provides protection from weather and road debris. Capacity may be more limited.

Prepare Your Car for Interstate Shipping

  • Wash the exterior and photograph the vehicle from multiple angles.
  • Record existing damage before pickup.
  • Remove valuables and personal belongings; carrier policies may restrict items in the vehicle.
  • Remove or deactivate toll transponders.
  • Confirm that the vehicle starts, rolls, steers, and brakes, or disclose inoperability.
  • Tell your coordinator about oversized dimensions, roof racks, modifications, low clearance, or special loading needs.
  • Keep enough fuel for loading and unloading; ask for the carrier’s preferred fuel level.
  • Ensure you or an authorized representative can inspect and sign the Bill of Lading.

Interstate Car Shipping FAQs

How do I ship a car to another state?

Request a quote with your route, vehicle details, preferred dates, and open or enclosed transport preference. After you review the available options and booking terms, a carrier can be assigned based on availability and shipment requirements.

How much does it cost to ship a car to another state?

Cost depends on distance, vehicle type, route demand, pickup and delivery access, transport type, season, timing, and carrier availability. Use the calculator for an estimate and request a quote for route-specific options.

How long does state-to-state car shipping take?

Transit time depends on distance, scheduling, weather, traffic, loading stops, and carrier operations. Your coordinator provides an estimated pickup window and transit timeframe based on your route.

Is open or enclosed transport better for interstate moves?

Open transport is commonly selected for standard vehicles. Enclosed transport provides added protection from weather and road exposure and is often selected for classic, luxury, exotic, collector, or condition-sensitive vehicles.

Do I need to be present for pickup and delivery?

You or an authorized representative should be available to review vehicle condition, sign the Bill of Lading, and release or receive the vehicle.

Can I ship an inoperable vehicle to another state?

Often, yes. Tell your transport specialist if the vehicle does not start, roll, steer, or brake so appropriate loading options can be reviewed.

Can I put personal belongings in my car?

Carriers primarily transport vehicles, not household goods. Policies vary by carrier and may limit personal items by weight, placement, insurance, and applicable regulations. Remove valuables and ask about the assigned carrier's policy before pickup.

What happens if the carrier cannot access my street?

The carrier and coordinator work with you to select a nearby safe, legal, truck-accessible meeting point.

Is Neon Auto Transport a broker or carrier?

Neon Auto Transport is a licensed auto transport broker. We arrange shipments through independently owned motor carriers that physically transport the vehicle.
