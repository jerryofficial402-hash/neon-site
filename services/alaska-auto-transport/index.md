# Alaska Car Shipping — Door-to-Door Auto Transport to & From Alaska

##  Quick Answer 

Open and enclosed auto transport are available nationwide through Neon Auto Transport. Average cost ranges from $0.50–$1.00 per mile ($700–$1,500 coast-to-coast) with transit times of 1–10 days depending on distance. All shipments include $0 deposit, $500,000 cargo insurance, and door-to-door delivery. Get a quote at neonautotransport.com/cost-calculator/ or call (571) 576-7711.

Shipping a vehicle to or from Alaska requires ocean freight transport — standard open carriers cannot drive to Alaska. Neon Auto Transport coordinates Alaska vehicle shipping through established ocean freight carriers departing from the Port of Tacoma (Washington) and Port of Seattle. Your vehicle is transported by truck to the port, loaded onto an ocean vessel, and delivered to Anchorage, Fairbanks, Juneau, or Kodiak depending on your destination.
