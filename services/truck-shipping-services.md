## Truck Shipping FAQs

What types of trucks can Neon Auto Transport ship? +

We ship all types of trucks including pickup trucks (Ford F-150, RAM, Silverado), heavy-duty trucks (F-250, 2500HD), commercial trucks, box trucks, and even lifted trucks. Oversized trucks may require specialized flatbed carriers. 

Does it cost more to ship a truck compared to a car? +

Yes, trucks typically cost 10-30% more to ship than standard sedans due to their larger size and heavier weight. Lifted trucks or dually trucks may incur additional surcharges of $100-$300 for requiring specialized equipment. 

Can you ship a truck with a lifted suspension or oversized tires? +

Yes, but lifted trucks over 7 feet tall may not fit on standard carriers. We use flatbed or step-deck trailers for oversized trucks. Provide your truck exact height and weight measurements when requesting a quote. 

Can I ship a truck with items in the bed? +

Items in the truck bed must be removed or secured below the bed rail height. Loose items are not covered by carrier insurance and can shift during transit causing damage. We recommend an empty bed for safest transport. 

How do I prepare my truck for auto transport? +

Remove aftermarket accessories (light bars, antennas, bed covers if loose), ensure fuel is at 1/4 tank, disable alarms, and document existing damage. Retracted towing mirrors and remove any roof-mounted accessories that increase height.

## Tips & Tricks for Saving on Vehicle Shipping

### Pro Tip for Truck Shipping Services

Provide exact dimensions and weight to avoid delays at pickup.

### Flexible Pickup & Delivery

Choosing a five-day window for pickup and delivery can lower your shipping cost. Carriers can plan routes more easily with flexible dates, which means more options and better pricing for you.

### Hub City Advantages

For your auto transport needs, choose a location near major highways. Spots close to major interstates attract more carriers, giving you more options and more competitive rates on your shipment.

### Open Trailers

Open trailers cost less than enclosed trailers and work well for most standard vehicles. If your car doesn’t need extra protection from the elements, an open trailer is the smart, budget-friendly choice.
