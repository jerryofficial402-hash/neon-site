## What Is Heavy Haul Transport?

Heavy haul transport refers to shipping loads that exceed standard legal size or weight limits for regular trucking — generally anything wider than 8.5 feet, taller than 13.6 feet, longer than 53 feet, or heavier than 80,000 pounds combined gross weight. Because these loads don't fit on a standard trailer or under normal road restrictions, they require specialized equipment, route planning, and state permits before they can move. 

### This category typically covers:

  * ✓ **Construction machinery** – excavators, bulldozers, backhoes, cranes, motor graders
  * ✓ **Agricultural equipment** – combines, tractors, harvesters
  * ✓ **Industrial machinery** – generators, transformers, compressors
  * ✓ **Oversized commercial vehicles** – dump trucks, tank trucks, mining equipment
  * ✓ **Specialty loads** – mobile offices, large tanks, and non-standard freight



If your shipment falls outside standard vehicle dimensions, it almost certainly qualifies as a heavy haul move — and needs a carrier equipped to handle it correctly.

Step Deck (Drop Deck) | Taller equipment needing extra vertical clearance | Up to ~48,000 lbs

Choosing incorrectly here isn't just inefficient — it can put your equipment, the driver, and other motorists at risk. That's why this decision is made by dispatchers who understand load dynamics. 

## Permits, Regulations, and Route Planning

Heavy haul shipments are governed by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA), along with individual state Departments of Transportation. Requirements vary widely from state to state. 

  * 1

**Oversize/overweight permits** for every state along the route

  * 2

**Pilot car or escort requirements** , which kick in for loads over certain thresholds

  * 3

**Route surveys** to identify low bridges, weight-restricted roads, and narrow lanes

  * 4

**Travel time restrictions** , since many states prohibit oversized loads moving at night




Neon Auto Transport's dispatch team handles this permitting and route-planning process on your behalf, keeping your shipment fully compliant.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What qualifies as a heavy haul load? +

Generally, any shipment wider than 8.5 feet, taller than 13.6 feet, longer than 53 feet, or heavier than 80,000 pounds gross weight is considered a heavy haul load and requires special permitting. 

Do I need a permit to ship heavy equipment? +

In most cases, yes. Oversize and overweight permits are typically required in every state your shipment passes through. Neon Auto Transport handles this process for you. 

What types of equipment can you transport? +

We ship construction machinery, agricultural equipment, industrial machinery, oversized commercial vehicles, and other non-standard loads that exceed typical shipping dimensions. 

How long does heavy haul transport take? +

Transit time depends on distance, route restrictions, and permit processing, but most domestic heavy haul shipments are completed within a similar timeframe to standard long-distance freight — typically a few days to just over a week.
