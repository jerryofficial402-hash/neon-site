Open vs. Enclosed Transport

When shipping a car across state lines, you have two primary carrier options. 90% of customers choose open transport, but enclosed is vital for specific vehicles.

🛣️ Open Transport

  • ✅ Cost-effective: 30-40% cheaper than enclosed.
  • ✅ Faster Pickup: Far more carriers available.
  • ⚠️ Exposed: Vehicle is exposed to weather and road debris.
  • 👉 Best for: Daily drivers, used cars, standard SUVs.

🛡️ Enclosed Transport

  • ✅ Protection: 100% shielded from weather/debris.
  • ✅ White-glove: Hydraulic lift gates prevent scraping.
  • ⚠️ Cost: More expensive, fewer carriers.
  • 👉 Best for: Classics, exotics, luxury, sentimental.

Major US Shipping Corridors

Where you are shipping to and from heavily impacts carrier availability and pricing. Highways with massive traffic volumes generally have faster pickups and more competitive rates.

The I-95 Corridor (East Coast)

Route: New York ↔ Florida
This is one of the busiest auto transport routes in the world. Carrier availability is massive, meaning prices are usually very competitive, except during Snowbird season.

The I-10 Corridor (Southern Route)

Route: California ↔ Texas ↔ Florida
A high-volume cross-country route that avoids snow and ice entirely. It remains highly active and relatively fast year-round.

The I-80 / I-90 Corridor (Northern Route)

Route: California ↔ Chicago ↔ New York
A massive cross-country artery. During the summer, it's fast and efficient. During the winter, expect significant weather delays, ice storms, and sudden price hikes.

How Seasons Affect Pricing

☀️ Summer Rush (May – August)

The busiest time of the year for the auto transport industry. Families relocating before school starts and college students moving push demand to its peak. Prices are generally 10-15% higher.

❄️ Snowbird Season (October & April)

In October/November, thousands ship cars from the Northeast down to Florida and Texas. Rates heading South skyrocket. In April/May, the reverse happens as they head back North.

🌨️ Winter Weather Delays (Dec – Feb)

Snow, ice, and reduced daylight hours slow down trucks traversing the northern and midwestern states. Carriers may charge a premium to navigate dangerous icy passes through the Rockies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my vehicle insured during state-to-state transport? +
Yes. By federal law, all carriers must carry active cargo insurance. Your vehicle is fully insured from the moment it is loaded onto the truck until it is safely delivered and signed off.
Can I put personal items in my car when shipping it to another state? +
Generally, carriers prefer the car to be empty to comply with DOT weight regulations. However, most allow up to 100 lbs of soft goods (blankets, clothes) secured in the trunk. Nothing can be placed in the front seats or block the windows.
How far in advance should I book my cross-country shipment? +
We recommend booking 1 to 3 weeks in advance. This gives our logistics team ample time to secure the best carrier on your route at the lowest possible price. However, expedited shipping is available if you need it moved immediately.
Can I ship a non-running vehicle to another state? +
Yes, but you must notify us in advance. Non-running vehicles require a specialized carrier equipped with a winch to load and unload the car safely. This typically adds $100-$150 to the total cost.
Do I need to be present for pickup and delivery? +
Either you or a designated representative (friend, family member, neighbor) must be present at both pickup and delivery to hand over the keys, inspect the vehicle, and sign the Bill of Lading.
How do I pay for my state-to-state car shipment? +
Neon Auto Transport requires a small reservation fee to dispatch the carrier, which is paid securely online via credit card. The remaining balance is paid directly to the driver at delivery using cash, cashier's check, or money order.
What is a Bill of Lading (BOL)? +
The BOL is a legal document that serves as a receipt for your vehicle and a record of its condition before and after transport. You must inspect your car and sign this document at both pickup and delivery.
How do I prepare my car for interstate shipping? +
Wash your car, remove all personal items and toll tags, disable the alarm, and ensure the gas tank is no more than 1/4 full to save on weight.
Are your auto transport quotes guaranteed? +
Yes, we provide transparent, guaranteed pricing with zero hidden fees. The price you see is the price you pay, assuming the vehicle details provided are accurate.
What happens if my car is damaged during transport? +
While extremely rare, if damage occurs, you must note it on the Bill of Lading at the time of delivery. We will then guide you through the process of filing a claim with the carrier's insurance, which covers 100% of transport-related damage.
Do you offer tracking for my interstate shipment? +
Yes. We provide regular updates via email and SMS. You can also call our dispatch team at any time for a real-time location update directly from the driver.
What is door-to-door car shipping? +
Door-to-door means the truck will come as close to your specified pickup and delivery addresses as safely and legally possible. If you live on a narrow street, you may need to meet the driver in a nearby large parking lot.
Why is Florida to New York more expensive in the Spring? +
This is 'Snowbird Season'. Thousands of retirees are shipping their cars back North at the same time, causing a massive spike in demand and carrier rates.
Is enclosed transport worth the extra cost? +
If you are shipping a classic, exotic, luxury, or highly sentimental vehicle, enclosed transport is highly recommended. It protects the car from weather, dust, and road debris. For daily drivers, open transport is perfectly safe and much cheaper.
Do I have to register my car immediately in my new state? +
Most states give you a grace period of 30 to 90 days to register your vehicle after establishing residency. Check your specific state's DMV website for exact deadlines and requirements.
