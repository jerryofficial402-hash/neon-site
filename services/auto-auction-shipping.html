Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you pick up a vehicle? +
Most pickups happen within 1–5 business days from auctions like Manheim, Copart, IAA, and ADESA. Expedited 24–48 hour pickup is available.
What do I need to provide? +
Your auction house name and location, buyer/lot number, gate release or pickup authorization, and a copy of your title or bill of sale.
Can you ship a salvage or non-running vehicle? +
Yes. We regularly ship salvage-title and non-running vehicles using winches or forklifts. A non-running surcharge of $100–$300 applies.
Do you ship collector vehicles? +
Yes. We recommend enclosed transport for collector purchases (Mecum, Bring a Trailer), providing full protection from weather and road debris.
