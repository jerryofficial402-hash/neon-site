Arizona Car Shipping: The Complete Guide to Cost, Routes, and Choosing a Carrier
Reviewed by the Neon Auto Transport Operations Team. Neon Auto Transport is a licensed auto transport brokerage operating under USDOT 4355879 and MC 1703787, shipping vehicles nationwide to and from Arizona.

Whether you’re relocating to Phoenix, sending a car to a college student in Tucson, or heading south for the winter as a snowbird, Arizona car shipping is one of the most requested transport corridors in the country. Arizona’s growing population, military bases, retiree communities, and year-round sunshine make it a constant hub for inbound and outbound vehicle transport.

This guide breaks down real cost ranges, the difference between open and enclosed transport, the most popular Arizona auto transport routes, and what to look for in a licensed carrier — so you can book with confidence.

Why People Ship Cars To and From Arizona

Arizona auto transport demand comes from a few consistent sources:

  • Snowbird relocations — retirees moving between Arizona and colder states each fall and spring
  • Military moves — Luke Air Force Base, Davis-Monthan AFB, and Fort Huachuca all generate steady vehicle shipping needs
  • College students — Arizona State University, University of Arizona, and Northern Arizona University bring students (and their cars) in and out of state each semester
  • Retirees and second-home owners in Scottsdale, Sun City, and Green Valley
  • Corporate relocations tied to Arizona’s growing tech and manufacturing sector
  • Online vehicle purchases shipped from dealerships or private sellers out of state

How Much Does Arizona Car Shipping Cost?

Car shipping cost is based primarily on distance, vehicle size, transport type (open vs. enclosed), time of year, and current carrier availability along your route. As a general rule, longer routes cost less per mile than shorter ones, because carriers can fill more of their trailer capacity efficiently.

The tables below give estimated ranges for a standard sedan on open transport. Enclosed transport typically runs 40–60% higher.

Most Arizona vehicle shipping customers choose open transport — it’s the same method dealerships use to move new cars and is safe for the vast majority of vehicles. Enclosed car shipping is worth the added cost for classic cars, luxury vehicles, or any car where paint and finish protection is a priority — especially relevant in Arizona given how common dust storms (haboobs) can be along desert interstates.

What Affects Arizona Car Shipping Pricing

  • Distance — the biggest single factor
  • Vehicle size and weight — trucks and SUVs cost more than compact cars
  • Transport type — open vs. enclosed
  • Season — snowbird season (fall/spring) increases demand on Arizona routes
  • Route popularity — high-traffic corridors like I-10 tend to be cheaper due to carrier availability
  • Vehicle condition — inoperable vehicles typically require special equipment and cost more
  • Pickup/delivery flexibility — flexible dates typically lower cost.

How to Choose a Licensed Arizona Auto Transport Company

Before booking any Arizona vehicle shipping service, verify:

  1. FMCSA licensing — Confirm the company’s USDOT and MC numbers are active on the FMCSA SAFER database.
  2. Insurance coverage — Ask for proof of cargo insurance, not just liability insurance.
  3. Transparent pricing — Be cautious of quotes far below the market range; they often lead to price increases after booking.
  4. Real customer feedback — Look for reviews across multiple platforms, not just the company’s own site.
  5. Broker vs. carrier — Understand whether you’re booking directly with a carrier or through a licensed broker who dispatches to a network of vetted carriers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to ship a car to Arizona?

Most car shipping to Arizona costs between $250 and $1,800, depending on the distance, vehicle type, and whether you choose open or enclosed transport. Short routes like California to Arizona typically fall on the lower end, while cross-country routes from the East Coast fall on the higher end.

How long does Arizona car shipping take?

Most routes take between 1 and 7 days. Short routes like Nevada to Arizona can take as little as a day, while cross-country routes from New York or Florida typically take 5 to 7 days depending on weather and carrier scheduling.

Is open or enclosed car transport better for Arizona?

Open transport is the more affordable and commonly used option, suitable for most everyday vehicles. Enclosed transport costs more but is recommended for classic, luxury, or exotic cars, and offers extra protection against Arizona’s occasional dust storms.

Do I need to be present for pickup and delivery?

No, but someone authorized should be available to inspect the vehicle and sign the bill of lading at both pickup and delivery.

Can I ship a car to Arizona that doesn’t run?

Yes, most auto transport companies can ship inoperable vehicles, but it typically requires special loading equipment and may cost more than shipping a running vehicle.

What is the cheapest way to ship a car to Arizona?

Open transport on a popular, high-traffic route (like California to Arizona) with flexible pickup dates is generally the most affordable option.

Is Arizona car shipping cheaper in certain seasons?

Yes. Prices often rise during snowbird season (October–November and March–April) due to higher demand. Booking outside peak season, or booking a few weeks in advance during peak season, can help lower costs.
