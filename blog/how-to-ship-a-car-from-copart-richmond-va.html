How to Ship a Car from Copart Richmond VA: Complete 2026 Guide

By Neon Auto Transport September 1, 2026 6 min read ✓ Verified for 2026 Auction Protocols

Winning a salvage or clean-title vehicle at Copart Richmond is exciting, but arranging transport quickly is critical to avoid hefty yard storage penalties. Whether you bought a repairable vehicle for personal use, inventory for your dealership, or an export unit, this comprehensive guide explains how to release and ship your vehicle from Copart’s Sandston and Charles City facilities directly to your destination.

Quick Answer: How to Ship a Car from Copart Richmond VA

  • 1. Pay your invoice in full and wait for funds to clear (wire transfer, ePay, or debit).
  • 2. Download your Gate Pass PIN from your Copart Member account.
  • 3. Book a Copart-approved carrier (e.g., Neon Auto Transport) with your Lot # and Gate PIN.
  • 4. The carrier schedules a pickup appointment in the Copart Transportation App and loads your vehicle at the Sandston or Charles City yard.
  • 5. Your car is delivered door-to-door to your home, auto repair shop, dealership, or maritime port.

Step-by-Step Pickup Process

Auction pickups require stricter coordination than standard private vehicle shipping. Follow this 5-step release sequence:

  1. Complete Invoice Payment: Pay your vehicle invoice in full via secure wire transfer, ePay, or debit. Copart will not release any lot or generate release credentials until funds have 100% cleared.
  2. Generate Your Gate Pass PIN: In your Copart Member portal, navigate to your won lots and download the official Gate Pass / Buyer Release PIN. Confirm the yard address.
  3. Book an Auction Carrier: Partner with a specialized transport broker like Neon Auto Transport. Provide your Lot #, 6-digit Gate PIN, vehicle operable status, and delivery address.

How Copart Storage Fees Work

Copart grants a limited free storage window—typically 2 to 3 business days following the sale date (depending on your buyer membership tier). Once this grace period expires, daily storage charges accumulate rapidly at $30 to $50+ per day.

Concrete Storage Fee Example:

If your free storage window ends on Thursday at 5:00 PM and your carrier arrives for pickup on Monday morning, you could owe 3 to 4 days of storage charges ($120–$200+) plus weekend facility surcharges. Booking dispatch within 24 hours of payment ensures your carrier is scheduled well before fees trigger.

Shipping Inoperable & Damaged Vehicles

Over 60% of inventory sold at Copart Richmond consists of non-running, salvage, or collision-damaged units. Vehicle condition dictates the required carrier equipment:

  • Runs & Drives: Operable vehicles that steer, brake, and drive under their own power are loaded onto standard multi-car open auto transport carriers using steel ramps.
  • Rolls & Steers (Inoperable): Vehicles with mechanical or electrical faults whose tires hold air and steering functions. These require carriers equipped with an electric winch.
  • Severe Collision Damage / Missing Wheels: Immobile salvage requiring Copart heavy yard forklifts to load onto dedicated flatbeds.

Cost Impact: Non-running vehicles typically cost $100 to $300 more to ship than operable cars due to winch labor, specialized equipment, and extended yard loading time. When shipping salvage vehicles, always declare the exact operable status upfront so the carrier arrives properly equipped.

Richmond-Specific Pickup Tips

  • Confirm Your Specific Yard: Always verify whether your car is at Sandston (5701 Whiteside Rd) or Charles City (6300 Chambers Road) before booking to avoid driver rerouting fees.
  • Target Weekday Morning Pickups: Plan pickup appointments on weekday mornings when yard congestion is lowest and forklift operators are most accessible.
  • Port Delivery for Exporters: If you are exporting, notify your broker immediately so they can assign a TWIC-card carrier for direct port delivery to Baltimore, Norfolk, or Savannah.

Frequently Asked Questions: Shipping from Copart Richmond

How do I ship a car from Copart Richmond VA?

Pay your invoice in full, download your Gate Pass PIN from your Copart account, then book a Copart-approved carrier with your lot number and gate PIN. The carrier schedules a pickup appointment at the Sandston or Charles City yard and delivers your vehicle door-to-door.

How much does it cost to ship a car from Copart Richmond?

Most shipments from Copart Richmond range from $250 to $1,500+, depending on distance and vehicle condition. Short regional moves (DC, Northern VA) often fall between $250–$400, while long-distance runs to Florida or Texas can reach $950–$1,800+.

Can I ship a non-running car from Copart Richmond?

Yes. Most Copart vehicles are non-running or damaged. Carriers use winches, flatbeds, or yard forklifts to load inoperable cars from Copart Richmond and transport them safely to your location.

How fast can a carrier pick up from Copart Richmond?

Neon Auto Transport typically arranges same-day or next-day dispatch once your payment has cleared and your gate pass is issued. Faster pickup helps you avoid daily Copart storage fees.

Do I need to be present when the car is picked up from Copart?

No. As long as your carrier has the correct Gate Pass PIN, lot number, and authorization, they can pick up the vehicle without you being on-site. You’ll receive continuous updates from the carrier during transit.
