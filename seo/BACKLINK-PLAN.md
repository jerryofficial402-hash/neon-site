**Teen baatein saaf hain:**
1. **Links bahut kam hain** — 17 domains. Asal masla yehi hai.
2. **Sab homepage par** — 112 / 143. Route pages, state pages aur guides ko koi bahar ka link nahi (sirf MSN wale 2 pages).
3. **Anchor text pehle se natural hai** — "visit website", logo, brand naam. Koi keyword-stuffed anchor nahi, koi penalty ka khatra nahi.
4. **Purane owner ke spam links nazar nahi aaye** (gambling/products wale URLs 410 de rahe hain). Disavow ki zaroorat nahi.

## 2. Usool (rules)

- Link **khareedna, PBN, link exchange schemes, fake guest posts — nahi.** Google ki spam policy ke khilaf hai; naya kamzor domain hai, risk nahi lena.
- Fake reviews nahi, company ke naam se fake posts nahi.
- Anchor text: brand ("Neon Auto Transport") aur URL zyada, keyword kam — jo natural tareeqon se khud hota hai.
- Links ko pages mein baantna: homepage ke ilawa route pages, state pages, cost guide, open-vs-enclosed.
- Har naya link yahan neeche log karna.
