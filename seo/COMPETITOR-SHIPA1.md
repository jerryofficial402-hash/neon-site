## 3. Why they rank (most important first)

1. **Domain age and authority.** Eight years of links and brand searches. This is the biggest gap and it cannot be closed quickly.
2. **Reviews.** 720 reviews at 4.8, shown in Google's star snippet. More reviews means more trust and a higher click rate.
3. **Topic coverage.** 281 blog guides plus 66 state hubs plus 22 state pages tell Google they cover every route and every vehicle type.
4. **Head-term targeting on the homepage.** The title, H1 and H2s repeat the phrases people search ("car transport", "vehicle shipping", "USA").
5. **Freshness.** Blogs keep being updated (last update 16 Nov) and pages say "Last Updated: November 2026".

## 5. Don't copy

- Meta description naming competitors ("company like Roadrunner, Nexus"): misleading.
- Keyword-stuffed headings on every block: harder to read, and Google's helpful-content system penalizes it.
- Mass-produced thin route blogs. We noindexed 226 thin pages for this reason.
- Services Neon doesn't actually offer (heavy equipment, boats) unless the owner confirms them.
