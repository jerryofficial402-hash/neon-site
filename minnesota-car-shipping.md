## Factors that Affect Minnesota Shipping Prices

### Distance

The farther your car travels, the more you’ll pay. However, longer trips often cost less per mile than shorter ones.

### Route

Shipping along major highways is cheaper and faster. Rural or off-the-beaten-path routes cost more due to limited carrier access.

### Season

Summer and snowbird season drive prices up. Shipping during slower months, like fall or early spring, can save you money.

### Vehicle Type

Larger vehicles like SUVs and trucks take up more space and use more fuel. Expect to pay more than you would for a standard sedan.

### Traffic Patterns

Busy travel weeks, like Thanksgiving, can slow down delivery times. Booking during lighter traffic windows helps keep costs and delays down.

### Carrier Availability

During peak season, carriers fill up fast. You may wait longer for pickup, so book early to lock in your preferred dates.

### Open vs Enclosed

Open trailers are the most affordable option. Enclosed trailers offer extra protection but cost more — ideal for luxury or classic cars.

### Population Density

Shipping to or from a busy city can cost more. Dense urban areas have more traffic and tighter access for large carriers.

## Tips & Tricks for Saving on Vehicle Shipping

### Off Season Savings

Car shipping rates drop during the off-season. Even shifting your shipment by a few weeks can lead to real savings. Avoid peak moving seasons like summer and major holidays to get the best rates.

### Flexible Pickup & Delivery

Choosing a five-day window for pickup and delivery can lower your shipping cost. Carriers can plan routes more easily with flexible dates, which means more options and better pricing for you.

### Hub City Advantages

For pickups and drop-offs in Minnesota, choose a location near major highways. Spots close to I-35 or near Minneapolis attract more carriers, giving you more options and more competitive rates on your shipment.

### Open Trailers

Open trailers cost less than enclosed trailers and work well for most standard vehicles. If your car doesn’t need extra protection from the elements, an open trailer is the smart, budget-friendly choice.

## Minnesota Vehicle Transport FAQs

How do I prepare my vehicle for transit to/from MN? +

Clear out personal items and take detailed photos of your car's exterior and interior. Minnesota is famous for extreme winter weather, so make sure your antifreeze is topped off and your battery is fully charged. Keep your gas tank around a quarter full. 

Is my vehicle insured transporting from Minnesota? +

Yes, your vehicle is fully insured during transit. Carriers traveling the Land of 10,000 Lakes via I-35 or I-94 are legally mandated to hold active cargo insurance to cover any unexpected damage. 

How far out should I schedule my pickup service in Minnesota? +

We recommend scheduling 1–2 weeks in advance for most Minnesota transports. For the huge winter snowbird season heading to warm states like Florida or Arizona, booking 2–4 weeks ahead helps secure top carriers and specific dates. 

How do I pay for my auto transport? +

There are no upfront costs at booking. You simply provide a payment method to lock in your carrier. The balance can be paid online by card, or in person using cash or a certified check directly to the driver at delivery. 

Can I pack personal items inside my vehicle? +

Yes, you can pack up to 100 lbs of personal belongings in the trunk or securely below the window line. Remember, carriers are not liable for personal items, so ensure all valuables are removed. 

Do I need to be present at both pickup and delivery? +

Yes, you or a trusted adult must be available at both the pickup and delivery locations in Minnesota. This allows you to inspect the vehicle with the driver and sign the Bill of Lading, which acts as the official receipt. 

What's the difference between Open vs Enclosed Auto Transport? +

Open transport is the most economical method, but your car will be exposed to Minnesota's road salt and weather. Enclosed transport utilizes a fully covered trailer, providing ultimate protection against the elements, perfect for classic and luxury cars.
