      COST FACTORS

      What Affects Your Car Shipping Quote?

      These core factors determine the price you see when you request an auto transport quote:

      Route Distance & Lane Popularity

      Longer routes such as New York to Florida or Texas to California often have lower per-mile costs, while short regional moves may be more expensive per mile due to loading and scheduling time.

      Vehicle Type & Condition

      Sedans and compact cars use standard carrier space. SUVs, trucks, vans, and oversized vehicles require more room and increase your quote. Inoperable vehicles require winch service and special handling.

      Carrier Type: Open vs Enclosed

      Open car shipping quotes are ideal for everyday vehicles and budget-friendly moves. Enclosed car shipping quotes include full weather-shielded protection and zero exposure to road debris. For more details on carrier types, visit our vehicle transport services page.

      Pickup & Delivery Locations

      Quotes to and from major metro areas (e.g., Los Angeles, Dallas, Chicago, Miami) benefit from greater carrier availability. Rural or remote locations may require extra mileage or terminal coordination.

      Seasonality & Timing

      Demand spikes during summer moves, college relocations, and snowbird season, which can affect pricing on popular corridors.

      FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

      Car Shipping Quote FAQs

      Direct, authoritative answers to common questions about auto shipping quotes.

      How do I get a car shipping quote? +
      You can get a car shipping quote by entering your pickup and delivery ZIP codes or cities, vehicle details, and preferred carrier type (open or enclosed) into our online quote form or cost calculator. Our system instantly calculates a price range based on distance, vehicle size, carrier availability, and season.
      Is a car shipping quote free? +
      Yes. All car shipping quotes from Neon Auto Transport are completely free and carry no obligation to book. You can request multiple quotes, compare options, and decide when you’re ready to ship.
      How accurate is the online car shipping quote? +
      Our online quotes are based on live market data and recent carrier dispatches on your route. While your quote is an estimate until a carrier is assigned, it reflects realistic pricing and usually matches the final booked rate within a narrow range.
      How long is my car shipping quote valid? +
      Most quotes are valid for 3–7 days, depending on route demand and season. If your pickup dates change, we can refresh your quote and provide updated pricing.
      Do I have to pay a deposit to get a car shipping quote? +
      No. Neon Auto Transport does not charge deposits just to request a quote. A deposit may be taken only after you approve a carrier assignment and confirm your booking.
      Can I get a car shipping quote for multiple vehicles? +
      Yes. You can request quotes for multiple cars, SUVs, trucks, motorcycles, or classic vehicles on the same route. Multi-vehicle bookings may be eligible for combined pricing or priority dispatch.
      Can I get a car shipping quote near me? +
      Yes. When you search for “car shipping quote near me” and enter your ZIP code, we match carriers that service your local area and preferred destination, providing quotes for both local and long-distance routes nationwide.
