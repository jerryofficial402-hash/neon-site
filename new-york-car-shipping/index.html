How to Save on New York Car Shipping

  • Book early. Early bookings typically secure lower rates and more pickup date flexibility.
  • Choose open transport. It's the most cost-effective option for standard vehicles.
  • Ship in the off-season. Late fall through early spring typically sees lower demand and better pricing than peak summer months.
  • Stay flexible on pickup dates. A 3–5 day pickup window often lowers your quote compared to a fixed date.
  • Consider terminal-to-terminal. If you're near a hub, this can save $100–$300 over door-to-door.

How to Prepare Your Vehicle for New York Car Transport

  1. Wash your vehicle so any existing scratches or dents are easy to document at pickup.
  2. Remove all personal items — carriers are not liable for belongings left inside.
  3. Keep the fuel tank to about a quarter full to reduce weight.
  4. Take timestamped photos from all angles before pickup.
  5. Disable any aftermarket alarm systems to prevent them from triggering in transit.

Receiving Your Vehicle in New York

When your vehicle arrives, inspect it against your pre-shipment photos before signing anything. Check for any new damage, confirm all personal items and accessories are accounted for, and sign the Proof of Delivery only once you're satisfied with the vehicle's condition.

New York Car Shipping FAQs

How much does it cost to ship a car to or from New York?

Shipping a car to or from New York typically costs $450–$1,875 depending on distance, vehicle type, and season. Short regional routes (like New York to Virginia) start around $450–$650, while cross-country routes (like New York to California or Washington) run $1,350–$1,875.

How long does it take to ship a car to or from New York?

Transit time ranges from 1–3 days for short regional routes up to 6–8 days for cross-country shipments, depending on distance and carrier scheduling.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Yes. Every shipment includes coverage up to $500,000 per vehicle, with full details provided before pickup.

Do I need to be present at pickup and delivery?

Yes, either you or an authorized representative should be present at both pickup and delivery to inspect the vehicle and sign the required paperwork.

What's the difference between open and enclosed transport?

Open transport is the most affordable option and suits standard vehicles. Enclosed transport fully shields your vehicle from weather and road debris, making it a better fit for luxury, classic, or exotic cars.

Can I ship a car to New York City specifically?

Yes. We provide direct service to all five boroughs, as well as Long Island and the surrounding metro area, though tight city streets may require a nearby meeting point for larger carriers.
