## How a car gets from Alaska to California

Most cars leave Alaska by ship from Anchorage to the Port of Tacoma in Washington. From Tacoma, a carrier truck takes I-5 south to California. The other option is driving the Alaska Highway through Canada, which is long and depends on the weather.

This route is mostly military families leaving JBER, Eielson or Fort Wainwright, and Alaskans moving south for work or retirement.
