How to Ship a Car to Another State: Step-by-Step Guide & Costs (2026)

Quick Answer: To ship a car to another state: 1) Get quotes from licensed brokers (USDOT #4355879), 2) Select open vs. enclosed transport, 3) Schedule a flexible 3-5 day pickup window, 4) Complete the Bill of Lading condition inspection at pickup, and 5) Verify vehicle condition upon delivery. Interstate shipping typically costs $0.60 to $1.00 per mile and takes 2 to 7 days depending on total distance.

The 5-Step Process to Shipping a Vehicle Interstate

Shipping a car across state lines is simple when you understand the five core stages from quote to delivery:

1

Request & Compare Quotes

Enter pickup/delivery ZIP codes and vehicle details. Ensure quotes are binding and backed by a licensed FMCSA broker (verifiable on SAFER).

2

Book & Schedule Pickup Window

Select open vs. enclosed carrier and choose a 3-5 day pickup window. Zero deposit should be paid until a carrier is officially dispatched.

3

Vehicle Inspection & Bill of Lading

The driver inspects your vehicle for existing scratches and documents condition on the official Bill of Lading before loading.

4

Interstate Transit

Your car travels safely on a commercial car carrier. Direct driver contact and status updates are provided throughout transit.

5

Delivery & Final Inspection

The vehicle is unloaded at your destination. You review condition against the Bill of Lading and sign off upon delivery.

Vehicle Preparation Checklist

✓ Fuel Level: Leave 1/4 tank of gas to minimize transport weight while ensuring driveability for loading.
✓ Remove Personal Items: Clear all valuables, luggage, and loose items from the interior.
✓ Disable Toll Tags: Remove E-ZPass or toll transponders to prevent phantom toll charges during transit.
✓ Document Damage: Wash the vehicle and take clear photos of all sides before pickup inspection.
