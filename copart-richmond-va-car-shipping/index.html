How to Ship a Car from Copart Richmond VA

Follow these 5 steps to ensure prompt vehicle release and hassle-free transit.

1

Win the Auction and Complete Payment

Pay your Copart invoice in full using wire transfer, ePay, or debit. Copart will not release any vehicle until funds clear.

2

Obtain Your Gate Pass & Lot Details

Log into your Copart Member portal. Download the Gate Pass / Buyer Release PIN and confirm whether your vehicle is at the Sandston or Charles City yard.

3

Book Dispatch with Neon Auto Transport

Provide your Lot #, Gate PIN, vehicle condition (operable, rolls/steers, or immobile), and delivery address. We assign an FMCSA-authorized carrier.

4

Carrier Yard Check-In & Loading

The driver schedules an arrival window via the Copart Transportation App, inspects the vehicle (Bill of Lading), and safely loads it via ramp, winch, or forklift.

5

Direct Door-to-Door Delivery

Your vehicle is delivered directly to your home, auto repair shop, dealership, or shipping port with full cargo insurance coverage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I ship a car from Copart Richmond VA? ▼

To ship a car from Copart Richmond, first pay your invoice in full. Once payment clears, retrieve your gate pass and lot number from your Copart portal. Contact Neon Auto Transport with your vehicle condition and delivery address. Our team assigns an authorized carrier who schedules a pickup via the Copart Transportation App and delivers your vehicle door-to-door.

How much does it cost to ship a car from Copart Richmond? ▼

Costs vary based on distance and vehicle condition. Standard open transport ranges from $300 to $1,500 for most continental U.S. routes. Non-running vehicles requiring winch loading add $100–$250, while heavy-duty flatbed or long-haul transport ranges from $800 to $4,000+. Copart also charges a standard $50–$75 gate/loading fee.

How fast can you pick up from Copart Richmond? ▼

We prioritize same-day or 24–48 hour dispatch once payment has cleared and the gate pass is generated. Quick pickup prevents you from incurring Copart's daily storage charges ($30–$50+/day) after the free storage window expires.

Can you ship non-running cars from Copart Richmond? ▼

Yes. Over 60% of Copart auction vehicles are salvage or inoperable. We dispatch winch-equipped trucks for vehicles that roll and steer, and arrange forklift or yard crane assistance at Copart Richmond for units with broken axles, frame damage, or missing wheels.

Do I need a broker to ship from Copart Richmond? ▼

While not legally mandatory, partnering with an experienced auto transport broker like Neon Auto Transport ensures access to Copart-vetted carriers, manages gate clearance paperwork, avoids expensive yard storage penalties, and provides full cargo insurance oversight.

What documents do I need for Copart Richmond pickup? ▼

Your assigned carrier requires your Copart Buyer Number, Lot Number, and Gate Pass / Release PIN. The carrier will provide their DOT/MC credentials and driver ID during gate check-in at the Sandston or Charles City yard.

Can you ship multiple vehicles from Copart Richmond at once? ▼

Yes. We frequently arrange multi-car loads (2 to 9 vehicles) for auto dealers, body shops, and vehicle exporters. Consolidating lots into single-carrier transport reduces total freight costs by 10%–20%.
