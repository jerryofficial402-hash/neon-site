## How to Ship a Car from Copart Richmond VA

Follow this step-by-step process for a smooth pickup from Copart Richmond:

### Step 1 – Win the Auction and Complete Payment
After you win a vehicle at Copart Richmond (Sandston or Charles City yard):
1. Pay your invoice in full (wire transfer, CashierPay, credit/debit).
2. Wait for payment to fully clear in Copart’s system.
3. *Note: Your carrier cannot pick up the vehicle until Copart confirms payment.*

### Step 2 – Get Your Gate Pass and Lot Details
Once payment clears:
1. Log into your Copart account.
2. Go to the Payments Due / Vehicles section.

### What documents do I need for Copart Richmond pickup?
