Frequently Asked Questions: Shipping from Copart Richmond

Everything you need to know about vehicle gate release, dispatch appointments, towing fees, and delivery from Copart Richmond VA.

How do I ship a car from Copart Richmond VA?

To ship a car from Copart Richmond, first pay your auction invoice in full using wire transfer, ePay, or debit. Once funds clear, retrieve your Gate Pass / Buyer PIN and confirm your yard location (Sandston #42 or Charles City #134). Book dispatch with Neon Auto Transport — our team assigns an authorized carrier who schedules a pickup window via the Copart Transportation App and delivers your vehicle door-to-door.

How much does it cost to ship a car from Copart Richmond?

Most shipments from Copart Richmond range from $300 to $1,500+ depending on distance and vehicle condition. Short regional moves (DC, Northern VA) often fall between $250–$400, while long-distance runs to Florida or Texas can reach $950–$1,800+. Non-running vehicles typically cost $100–$300 more due to winch or forklift loading.

How fast can you pick up from Copart Richmond?

Neon Auto Transport typically arranges same-day or next-day dispatch once your payment has cleared and your gate pass is issued. Faster pickup helps you avoid daily Copart storage fees that start after the 2–3 business day free window.

Can you ship non-running cars from Copart Richmond?

Yes. Most Copart vehicles are non-running or damaged. Carriers use winches, flatbeds, or yard forklifts to load inoperable cars from Copart Richmond and transport them safely to your location.

Do I need a broker to ship from Copart Richmond?

No, you don’t legally need a broker, but using a broker like Neon Auto Transport saves time and reduces risk. We find Copart-approved carriers, handle gate pass coordination, and provide a single point of contact for tracking and issues.

What documents do I need for Copart Richmond pickup?

Your carrier needs your Gate Pass / Buyer PIN, lot number, and yard location (Sandston or Charles City). The carrier must be registered in Copart’s system with a valid MC number and insurance; Neon Auto Transport handles this setup for you.

Can you ship multiple vehicles from Copart Richmond at once?

Yes. If you’ve bought several vehicles from the Sandston or Charles City yards, we can consolidate them into a multi-car load and coordinate a single pickup window when possible. Volume pricing is available for dealers and rebuilders.
